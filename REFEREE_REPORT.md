## Major objections

**M1 — McMullen theorem applied outside the hypotheses documented.** Location: `sections/marking.tex:101-126`, primeness paragraph. McMullen, *Complex Dynamics and Renormalization*, Theorem 7.3 explicitly concerns **distinct small filled Julia sets of an n-renormalization of a quadratic polynomial**. The proof invokes it for an arbitrary putative period-two return of `G_p=f_{c_p}^p` (a polynomial of degree `2^p`), without proving the putative return is nested in the displayed quadratic-like restriction and that hybrid straightening transports its *full* pair of little sets. The asserted contradiction using two critical points of `G_p^2` also must place the nonzero inverse point **inside the proper degree-two return domain**, rather than just in an ambient filled set. These may be repairable, but the cited theorem does not supply those missing steps. **Remedy:** state primeness conditionally until nesting/domain containment is supplied.

**M2 — Wrong connectedness justification and unjustified boundary regularity.** Location: `sections/marking.tex:98-104, 151-165`. The global polynomial `G_p=f_{c_p}^p` has `2^p-1` critical points with multiplicity; trapping 0 in one central restriction is **not** Douady–Hubbard Proposition 2's criterion for the entire global map. One can instead use `K(f_{c_p})=K(f_{c_p}^p)` and the fact that the **unique critical orbit of `f_{c_p}` is bounded**. Also, a degree-two proper restriction and a Rouché count do **not by themselves prove** the boundary of `V_p` is a Jordan curve. For a polynomial inverse image of a round disk, finite semialgebraic/lemniscate boundary local connectedness is a possible replacement; regularity at critical boundary values must be checked. **Remedy:** fix connectedness proof and do not claim Jordan regularity absent proof.

**M3 — The root and “uniform margin” verifier is a finite exact decision procedure only after auditing its algorithms.** Location: `sections/parameter_proof.tex:3-34, 40-218, 223-313`; `sections/reproduction.tex:68-225`. Finitary interval recurrences rigorously bracket values if all interval operators, derivative intervals, reduction and strict comparators are sound. The p≥13 tail is a **human analytic induction** (including cosine, `τ`-derivatives, full-complex-disk tail, subchart exclusions), not computationally checked for infinitely many p. In particular, the six “1000 subintervals” in the `τ` nonroot region establish only their precisely coded subclaims, and the proof must independently account for intervening windows and signed-itinerary exclusions. The sharp 3-return statement requires its separate normalized disk, not an inference from the first-return gap. **Remedy:** retain explicit *local* (not global parameter) uniqueness and distinguish executable rational gates from unformalized universal deductions. Do not call a successful unit test an independent mathematical certificate.

**M4 — A parent/child inequality is readily misread as a common-parent gain.** Location: `sections/wave_proofs.tex:652-704`; `main.tex:29-36`. KKL **Lemma A.11**, not Proposition A.12, is the harmonic-combination width inequality; it requires a leaf of *each* child lamination in every parent leaf's restriction, **plus** injectivity of the immersion on their union. Proposition A.12 is the separate combinatorial path-chain result, and only applies after the pulled marking is refined as required. The selected circular leaves have a special 2-to-1 split; independent `P_p` and `C_p^±` suprema are not co-attained and cannot be iterated as a WAD amplifier. The v1 proposition correctly records the opposite sign on pairable families, but the sandwich's `P_{p,2}` omits the proposition's explicit injectivity/designated-class clause and should be made identical or distinguished. **Remedy:** align the definitions; put the conditional and leafwise results adjacent and clearly separate.

**M5 — Misuse of de Melo–van Strien Theorem II.10.1 at depth two.** Location: `sections/buffer_depth.tex:431-482`, sentence starting “The first return is straightened to the designated suffix ...”. The cited theorem, section II.10, proves injectivity of the **real quadratic logistic parameter** under equality of complete kneading invariants for eventually periodic turning points. It neither supplies a hybrid straightening of an arbitrary nested polynomial-like restriction nor proves a finite list of signed orbit cuts equals the entire suffix kneading invariant. The latter is a further analytic/combinatorial lemma. **Remedy:** treat the nine charts as finite root/margin data and call the named suffix and corresponding KKL interpretation **conditional**.

**M6 — The buffer argument is not a WAD support determination.** Location: `sections/buffer_depth.tex:31-203`. The cyclic-cover upper estimate for an incident universal-cover ideal pair uses separation of the deck translates, peripheral stabilizer and the correct length/width convention. The program checks the algebraic threshold and ellipse containment but not hyperbolic uniformization or SC/Landen. Even accepting that argument, the six inner endpoint *types* can contain infinitely many classes; zero/positive buffered weights are undecided. **Remedy:** keep `p≥11` outer exclusions **conditional on the cyclic-cover lemma** and state no buffered amplifier. Additionally, the merged `tip_family_buffered_wad_inner_20261116` audit records KKL's qualitative support-cardinality bound `≤3|χ|+|I|=10` for each fixed p; it does **not** identify the classes or yield a uniform deck-word cutoff. Reconcile “infinitely many candidate classes” with “at most ten positive classes.”

**M7 — The abstract overstates the evidential status of an extremely long theorem chain.** Location: `main.tex:29-37` and `REVIEW_FINDINGS.md`, RF-03/RF-05/RF-11. The abstract grammatically announces fully proved physical KKL markings, width sandwich, and strictly positive independent gain for every p while the manuscript concedes that named analytic/continuum theorems are not independently reviewed and the code only checks rational premises. “No MLC result” is an appropriate disclaimer but not a cure for this mismatch. **Remedy:** lead the abstract with executable finite results, give conditional mathematical consequences and name open problems as open.

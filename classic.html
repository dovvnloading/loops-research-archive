A Marked-Width Reduction of Mandelbrot Local Connectivity

Matthew Robert Wesney
225 pages  ·  465 ledger obligations  ·  three-paper sequence
Main manuscript Papers 1–3 Proof ledger Research map Repository
Abstract

We develop a source-locked reduction of the Mandelbrot local connectivity conjecture to a marked backward transition theorem for mixed renormalization towers. The proposed state is a partially invariant virtual or pseudo-Siegel carrier with explicit postcritical, prime-end, obstacle, and curve-family records; an unmarked continuum is insufficient. We prove finite root-pullback and inverse-tree reductions, exact critical-value and attachment formulae, functorial finite routing, a common-domain theorem, finite Wave-horizon closure under stated hypotheses, and several source-locked KKL corrections and no-go results.

The stationary-Wave QA reconstructs the native proof line by line. It finds an unproved relative telescope before the separator argument, an undefined terminal index, a missing measurable final-exit lamination, and a false degree-one assertion; the canonical continuum degree is two. A cyclic-cover model also refutes the literal broad coefficient-one KKL Wave-pushforward statement under its printed definitions, while leaving the narrower Dudko–Lyubich one-common-interval annular theorem intact. The native repair is therefore split into four explicit gates: relative continuation, pointwise two-path breaking, measurable final fixed-component exit, and typed bounded-degree pushforward. The present manuscript proves the relative-continuation gate; the other three remain open.

We formulate the remaining construction, transition, compactness, and rigidity requirements as the Universal Protected-Tower Compactness and Rigidity Proposition and prove its implication to MLC only conditionally. That proposition, VAAPC-I, and MLC remain open; this manuscript does not claim an unconditional proof of Mandelbrot local connectivity.

Status  Nothing here claims MLC. One implication is proved conditionally; the hypotheses it rests on are open, and the ledger below names every one of them.

I

The marked state

For $f_c(z)=z^2+c$ and $\mathcal{M}=\{c\in\mathbb{C}: (f_c^n(0))_{n\ge 0} \text{ bounded}\}$, the target is local connectivity of $\mathcal{M}$ at every parameter $c\in\mathcal{M}$. At a renormalization checkpoint $n$ the proposed state is not a continuum alone but

$$\mathcal{H}_n=\bigl(\Sigma_n,\;H_n,\;B_n,\;P_n^{\le \nu_n},\;I_n,\;\Gamma_n\bigr),\qquad \mathcal{D}^{\#}_n=\mathrm{Ext}_{\Sigma_n\setminus(H_n\cup B_n\cup P_n^{\le\nu_n})}(\Gamma_n).$$

Here $\Sigma_n$ is the ambient marked surface, $H_n$ the regularized carrier, $B_n$ the satellite base-cycle or neutral access data, $P_n^{\le\nu_n}$ the finite critical orbit actually used by the proof, $I_n$ the ideal or sectorial boundary marking, and $\Gamma_n$ the declared curve family. Neither existence nor uniform control of the state over all towers is assumed.

Why root modulus cannot be the coordinate

The BLOT satellite bound $\operatorname{mod}(A)<2\pi/\log(4(s+1))$ forbids any bounded scalar functional $D$ with $|D-1/\operatorname{mod}(A)|\le C$ uniformly over satellite relative periods $s$ (Theorem 4.1). This does not prohibit a regularized marked functional — it says the marked surface and curve family must genuinely change. Renaming the ordinary root annulus cannot solve the satellite problem.

The scalar closure is then supplied by the backward-record lemma: if $L_n>D_*$ forces some $p(n)1$, then $\sup_n L_n \le \max\{D_*,L_0\}+C/(\lambda-1)$. Forward growth $L_{n+k}\ge \lambda L_n - C$ is not a contradiction; the direction needed for tower closure is the reverse one.

II

Proved here

Unconditional within their stated finite topological or arithmetic hypotheses.

§5Theorem 1.3. Root-pullback carrier equivalence. One full root continuum, together with the routed components of all its finite iterated pullbacks containing the complete required packages, determines a unique finite saturated carrier forest. Selected sheets are distinct exactly when their packages lie in distinct same-depth pullback components.

§5Theorem 1.4. Critical-value corridor criterion. For a quadratic polynomial, a selected pullback edge over a full target carrier has degree one exactly when the target avoids the critical value, and degree two exactly when it contains the critical value — regardless of the label the target carries.

§6.1Theorem 1.5. Endpoint-birth accounting. For successive boundary coverings of degrees $d_j$, terminal endpoint count $p_N$, and $b_j$ genuinely new endpoints born at stage $j$,

$$p_0=\Bigl(\prod_{j=0}^{N-1} d_j\Bigr)p_N+\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}\Bigl(\prod_{i=0}^{j-1} d_i\Bigr)b_j.$$

§6.2Theorem 1.6. Attachment-support reduction. Under the exact KKL-form interval pullback description, genuine endpoint births vanish away from components of the pulled-carrier excess attached to the marked interval. Births confined to a bounded terminal quadratic window have a period-independent degree-weighted cost.

§6.4 · no-goTheorem 1.7. Canonical-carrier obstruction. No least connected full carrier exists even for a two-point package. Moreover an explicit critical-value-free branch of $z^2$ has a first pulled excess whose monotone absorption erases a protected upper prime-end access while producing no sheet, critical, obstacle, or localization collision.

§10.4Theorem 1.8. Protected free-component criterion. After the complete finite protected-collar and obstacle data are frozen, a protected connector exists exactly when one component of their complement has simultaneous singleton ports to every required full atom. A protected separator between two required port classes is an exact obstruction inside the current localization.

§10.5Theorem 1.9. Augmented-atom common domain. If the frozen forbidden inventory is covered by finitely many pairwise disjoint full augmented atom/obstacle packets compactly contained in one Jordan localization, and every original atom retains an explicit free singleton port, then all ports lie in one component of the original free domain. The proof uses Alexander duality for arbitrary compacta and assumes no local connectivity.

§10.7Theorems 1.11–1.12. Horizon-first inventory and the resolved Wave compiler. Dependency reachability from the complete proof-use seed set is the unique least inventory. A finite acyclic analytic Wave program with actual marked branches, exact degree words and stability proofs has a finite maximal iterate horizon and an order-independent, analytically stable least final-root closure. KKL's native recursion is a positive control with forbidden critical-visit chain length $216b^2$; it does not construct the marked virtual correspondence.

§10.9–10.10Theorems 1.13–1.14. First decoration-scale selector and terminal Hubbard-tree anchor. For a fixed non-principal decoration outside a directly attached satellite copy $\mathcal{M}_{p/q}$, the least critical $q$-return escape $n$ selects the actual puzzle depth $\nu_{\mathrm{dec}}(1)=nq+1$; the terminal primitive little-Julia cycle does not fit in one critical return base, so the analytic replacement is a multi-base return chain. In KKL's native category the terminal cycle's compact forward-invariant Hubbard tree is a connected carrier, and every finite selected vertex package has a unique convex subtree.

§10.11 · no-goTheorem 1.15. Cantor-package finite-trace criterion. An infinite family of distinct virtual small-Julia components cannot be identified injectively with a finite terminal-tree package. A finite quotient supports a declared joint observable exactly when that observable is constant on every quotient fiber; copy-chain labels alone construct neither an embedded component family nor an analytic branch action.

§12Theorem 1.17. Normalized end tools, full-trace calculus, collar obstruction. Finite-cylinder copies along one infinite direct-decoration address have a normalized direct limit canonically biholomorphic to the root, and fully transported extremal-length and electrode-response data are conformally invariant. Scalar nodal response nevertheless fails: on a length-$L$ cylinder the constant conductance is $2\pi/L$, while the $\cos(k\theta)$ trace carries energy

$$\pi k \coth(kL).$$

§8Theorems 8.3–8.5. Future-observable compactification. Countably many partial deterministic transitions with a frozen countable observable catalog admit a compact metrizable future-faithful end topology on which every declared guard, hull encoding, branch, transport and authorized enlargement extends continuously. It removes bare symbolic discreteness; it does not compactify the virtual hull geometry.

III

The four gates

The repair of the native stationary-Wave argument splits into four independent gates. One is closed.

N1  Relative continuation

Proved · Thm 12.101

Borel leafwise-directed stationary continuation. For $b\ge 1$ and $N=b+3$ there are nested proper Borel subwaves $A_0\supset\cdots\supset A_{N-1}$ whose output waves $S_n=F^n_*A_n$ retain the no-short, unbreakability, occurrence, prime-end marking and leafwise direction data, with $W(A_n\setminus A_{n+1})\le C_b$ independent of the rotation denominator, the input width, the number of represented arc packets and all selector histories. The native Dudko–Lyubich annular argument is applied only to the rectifiable core; the non-rectifiable remainder has zero width.

N2  Pointwise two-path breaking

Open

The separator argument requires breaking on two paths pointwise, not merely on an aggregate family. The native text asserts it before proving a relative telescope.

N3  Measurable final fixed-component exit

Open

The final-exit lamination is used but never constructed measurably, and the terminal index it is indexed by is undefined in the printed argument.

N4  Typed bounded-degree pushforward

Open

A usable coefficient-one theorem must prove single-crest, single-WAD-coordinate coherence for the actual source packet. Otherwise the state must retain the equivalence classes of source conductance coordinates that collapse downstairs and charge their multiplicity linearly.

IV

Corrections

An audit of one printed source line, run against the locked source file. It proves no global closure theorem.

Theorem 12.100. Cyclic-cover obstruction to the broad Wave claim. Under the broad printed definition of a KKL Wave, the coefficient-one estimate

$$W(A')\le W(A)+O(1+\log d),\qquad A\subset f_*A'$$

is false without an additional coherence or nonrepetition hypothesis.

Proof. Take a finite cyclic annulus cover and $N$ disjoint lifts of one proper marked target Wave of width $w$; if necessary add one smooth outer leaf of width zero to force one connected source base. The finite Parallel Law gives $W(A')=Nw$, while all positive-width lift components have the same set-theoretic pushforward, so every target Wave $A\subset f_*A'$ has $W(A)\le w$. The covering degree is at most the fixed integer $M=N+2$, so the estimate would imply $(N-1)w\le C(1+\log M)$, which fails for $N=2$ and $w\to\infty$. □

This refutes the candidate full-base collision reduction and the literal broad wording of the KKL Wave lemma under its printed definitions. It does not refute the cited Dudko–Lyubich annular theorem, whose source family is one Wave above one fixed boundary interval.

Findings of the line-by-line reconstruction

  1. iAn unproved relative telescope stands before the separator argument.
  2. iiA terminal index used in the argument is undefined.
  3. iiiThe measurable final-exit lamination is missing.
  4. ivA degree-one assertion is false: the canonical continuum degree is two.

Nineteen adversarial tests (A1–A19, §14.3) record the failure modes any completion must survive: root shadow split, late merger, hidden attachment, access explosion, relative-cover failure, horizon leakage, chart-cycle neutrality, rigidity circularity, trace-mode loss, and others. An open obligation may be promoted only with an exact quantified statement, a complete proof or a source with matching hypotheses, every constant dependence, an attack aimed at every hypothesis, its position in the dependency graph, and a successful build audit.

V

Conditional endpoint

Constants $d_0,w_0,m_0\in\mathbb{N}$, $\rho_0,\kappa_0,T_0,D_*,E_0<\infty$, $\eta_0>0$, $\lambda_{\mathrm{raw}}>1$ and $0\le\delta<\lambda_{\mathrm{raw}}-1$ are chosen before the polynomial. The Universal Protected-Tower Compactness and Rigidity Proposition asserts that every infinitely renormalizable quadratic polynomial admits canonical checkpoints $n$ with predecessors $p(n)

  1. UPTCR-1  Canonical protected carrier. Built from a lamination-lifted regulated tree or rooted predecessor puzzle-cell tree, containing every object used by Wave, Wanderers, quasi-additivity, covering, response or width arguments through the complete horizon. Every realized protected core, including every central degree-one corridor, has collision invariant $\chi_n=0$.

  2. UPTCR-2  Exact analytic realization. At every stage of a complete Wave horizon, $g_j^{-1}(H_{j+1})=H'_j=\iota_j^{-1}(H_j)$. The selected return spine has exactly one declared degree-two stage; every auxiliary branch carries its full critical word with $\deg(\beta)=2^{m(\beta)}$, and all markings transport equivariantly with preserved cyclic order.

  3. UPTCR-3  Uniform finite response state. Core and WAD modules of dimension at most $d_0,w_0$ with $\rho_n\le\rho_0$ and $\kappa_n/r_{B,n}\le\kappa_0$, future-observable for all required KKL Value functionals. No ordinary satellite-root modulus is used uniformly in the relative period.

  4. UPTCR-4  Backward transition. One calibrated potential with $L_n>D_*\Rightarrow L_{p(n)}\ge(\lambda_{\mathrm{raw}}-\delta)L_n-E_0$; every complete block certified by entropy gain at least $\eta_0$, by controlled pseudo-Siegel absorption, or by a proved transient/shear alternative, with total error charge $G_B(e_B)\le\delta L_n+E_0$.

  5. UPTCR-5  Coercive marked compactness. Bounded $L_n$ makes normalized marked towers sequentially precompact in a topology retaining carrier, access, relative-cover, response and Value data. Unbounded counters and stack words are forgotten after proved block closure or embedded in a compact end topology.

  6. UPTCR-6  Tower rigidity and parameter fiber. Puzzle families of Teichmüller diameter at most $T_0$; equal full marked combinatorics give a limiting global quasiconformal conjugacy whose invariant Beltrami form vanishes for want of an invariant line field, so the quadratic parameters coincide.

Conditional Theorem 9.4

UPTCR $\Rightarrow$ MLC

Assuming H1 (marked construction), H2 (marked backward transition), H3 (coercivity) and H4 (parameter rigidity), $\mathcal{M}$ is locally connected; Yoccoz–Hubbard covers the non-infinitely-renormalizable side. A logical endpoint, not a proof of the hypotheses — H1, H2, H3, H4, UPTCR and MLC are all open.

VIII

Context

Statements below are from the cited sources; links go to the primary source.

1985

Douady–Hubbard pose MLC

The conjecture that $\mathcal{M}$ is locally connected; it would imply a precise topological model for $\mathcal{M}$ and the Fatou conjecture on density of hyperbolicity. The polynomial-like external construction of Douady–Hubbard is imported here as Theorem 2.5.

c. 1990

Yoccoz: everything not infinitely renormalizable

MLC at every parameter that is not infinitely quadratic-like renormalizable, written up by Hubbard in 1993 and imported here as Theorem 2.1. This tied the problem to quadratic-like renormalization theory and left the infinitely renormalizable side open.

1997–2009

A priori bounds: Lyubich, Kahn, Kahn–Lyubich

Lyubich reduced MLC, under a secondary limbs condition, to a priori bounds; Kahn (2006) proved bounds at all parameters of bounded primitive type, and the Decorations and unicritical local connectivity papers built the near-degenerate machinery that has dominated the field since.

2022–2025

Satellite side and Feigenbaum points

Dudko–Lyubich (2023) proved a priori bounds for Feigenbaum quadratics of bounded type, giving MLC at the classical period-doubling parameter and the complex tripling renormalizations. Blokh–Levin–Oversteegen–Timorin (2022) supplied the satellite root-annulus upper bound this manuscript uses to rule out any uniform scalar root-modulus coordinate.

2026

KKL: parabolically bounded primitive renormalization

Kahn, Kapiamba and Lyubich prove a priori bounds and MLC for a class of primitive parameters that can approach the cusp, developing the Thin-Thick Decomposition, the Value Calculus, the Wanderers Theorem and the Wave Lemma (arXiv:2606.27272). This manuscript's source lock, imports and corrections are stated against that text and Dudko's survey.

This work

A marked-width reduction

Reduces MLC to a marked backward transition theorem for mixed towers, proves the finite root-pullback and attachment calculus, closes the relative-continuation gate, and records exactly which construction, transition, compactness and rigidity statements remain open. MLC itself is untouched.

# Geometric fiber dynamics and invariant marked-continuum saturation

## Claim

Lean-verified results: full forward transport of connected geometric fibers under open closed physical dynamics and finite model point fibers; actual complex quadratic specialization; critical-value and two-sheet rigidity; exact recursively selected inverse-component transport; and an exact saturation obstruction for maximal invariant marks; full return-survivor correspondence under preperiodic frontier anchors; arbitrary singleton-cut-set transport; and a common-cut criterion for marked dynamical model identification; and full fiber dynamics on the actual bounded-orbit filled Julia set, with compactness and complete invariance proved internally.

## Evidence

[StationaryFiberDynamics.lean](../../formalization/weighted/StationaryFiberDynamics.lean) contains twenty public theorem declarations and two private helpers, with two additional definitions for the actual filled set and its map. The first sixteen public theorems and both private proofs were compiled and audited at commit [0f3f948](https://github.com/dovvnloading/loops-research-archive/commit/0f3f948dc6bcea57f204302218c2f7e521ea7cbd). All twenty public theorems and the two definitions are verified at [b2203a0](https://github.com/dovvnloading/loops-research-archive/commit/b2203a05c9c75cbb5b78c71feb9b6d7fcc753fd8). The compiled audit also includes private and compiler-generated declarations.

1. `fiber_image_eq_of_finite_model_fibers`: for a continuous surjective monotone semiconjugacy p and an open closed physical map F, the image of each fiber over y is relatively open and closed in the fiber over G(y), provided the model predecessor fiber is finite. Its relative openness follows by deleting all other model predecessors, a finite closed set. Connectedness of the target fiber and nonemptiness force equality.
2. `iterate_fiber_image_eq`, `singleton_fiber_forward`: the equality composes along every finite orbit and preserves singleton fibers forward. Combined with the existing backward theorem this identifies propagation in both directions when its finite physical fiber premise also holds.
3. `quadratic_restriction_open`, `quadratic_restriction_fiber_image`: the actual quadratic on a compact completely invariant subspace is open and closed in its relative topology. This supplies the physical dynamical input, rather than assuming that a restriction to a nonopen filled set is open.
4. `quadratic_model_critical_preimage`, `quadratic_two_model_predecessors_injective`: the actual zero critical point has a unique physical inverse at its critical value; whole-fiber transport forces a unique model predecessor there. If a model point has two different predecessors, each physical predecessor fiber maps injectively onto the common successor fiber: otherwise a third distinct root would be required. The algebra uses the actual factorization of the difference of squares.
5. `selected_pullback_chain_full_lift`: the full preimage equality propagates recursively along actual connected components, given only the base equality and the matching component anchors at each depth. This uses the existing exact connected-component lift, not an equality of abstract tree labels.
6. `invariant_trapping_mark_saturated`: a mark that contains all points staying forever in an open return region and is forward invariant inside that region is saturated if its model image is disjoint from the image of the region frontier. Any orbit of another point in a mark fiber that leaves the region forces its connected fiber to hit the frontier. Unlike the previous global frontier-singleton criterion, only fibers meeting this mark are constrained.

7. `invariant_trapping_mark_saturation_iff`: the frontier-image obstruction is also necessary, since a saturated mark inside an open region cannot share a fiber with a frontier point outside that region. This gives an exact criterion, not merely a sufficient one.
8. `preperiodic_frontier_trapping_correspondence`, `quadratic_return_preperiodic_frontier`: a frontier point eventually mapping to a known singleton fiber has singleton fiber by the earlier finite-physical-fiber lemma. The entire trapping region is therefore saturated. For each return period P, semiconjugacy gives exact full inverse-image and image identities for the set staying forever in the physical return region and the set staying forever in its model image. The quadratic version discharges the finite physical fiber assumption with the actual polynomial. Unlike whole-fiber dynamics, this correspondence does not require a finite-to-one model map. If the physical mark is identified with this maximal survivor and the specified model region is its actual image, the mark correspondence follows. These identifications are not silently assumed.

9. `singleton_cut_set_full_lift`: removes an arbitrary set of singleton-fiber physical anchors, with exact full preimage correspondence on the actual punctured components. This does not require local connectivity, a finite cut set, or open components.
10. `quotient_fiber_refinement_of_cut_separation`: let p and q be two geometric quotient maps with common singleton-fiber anchors A. For x,z in the same p-fiber, its connected q-image cannot cross any q(a) unless it contains that singleton anchor. Hence q(x) and q(z) have identical anchor equality and punctured-component tests. If these tests separate all points of the q-target, then the p-fiber equivalence refines the q-fiber equivalence. This is a one-sided criterion, not an assumption of equal equivalences.
11. `quotient_dynamical_homeomorphism_of_equal_fibers`, `quotient_dynamical_homeomorphism_of_common_cuts`: applying the refinement in both directions constructs a homeomorphism of the compact-source Hausdorff models, intertwining the two dynamics and carrying the image of **every physical subset** to its corresponding image. Both continuous inverse maps are constructed and checked. This reduces the geometric part of the first line of (6.1) in the Kiwi audit to explicit common singleton-anchor and punctured-component separation hypotheses; it does not identify the actual named anchors or external access markings by fiat.

12. `quadratic_filled_iff_bounded`, `quadratic_filled_compact`, `quadratic_filled_complete_invariant`, `quadratic_filled_fiber_image`: specialize to the actual quadratic filled Julia set at every complex parameter, using the repository's previously proved escape potential. The zero locus equals the genuine bounded-orbit locus; joint continuity of the escape potential and its uniform zero-orbit bound prove compactness, and its functional equation proves both directions of complete invariance. Thus the actual filled-set self-map has whole-fiber transport for the stated finite-to-one monotone semiconjugacy without assuming compactness or backward invariance of an unspecified carrier. The named KKL map and its marked straightening identification are still separate obligations.

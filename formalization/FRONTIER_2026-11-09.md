## 7. Proof acceptance protocol

For any proposed promotion, require: (i) a typed source object at the declared scale and clock, (ii) exact source theorem and hypotheses, (iii) a constructive theorem/countermodel with actual inputs, (iv) a Lean kernel proof of the *encoded* discrete part, (v) analytic topology/width proofs explicitly imported or formalized instead of hidden as an `axiom`, (vi) a trace to the relevant ledger ID/evidence and an independent geometry audit, and (vii) a status change only after that evidence exists. Never infer local connectivity from an abstract package whose predicates were filled with arbitrary Boolean truths.

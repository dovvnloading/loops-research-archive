## Satellite modulus import

**<a id="BLOT_NONPARABOLIC_LIMIT_MODULUS_COLLAPSE"></a>`BLOT_NONPARABOLIC_LIMIT_MODULUS_COLLAPSE` — primary proof located.** In Theorem B of Blokh, Levin, Oversteegen and Timorin the rational maps and their limit have the same degree $d\ge2$. The connected renormalization Julia sets converge in the Hausdorff metric to a compact set with at least two points, the renormalization periods tend to infinity, and no parabolic periodic domain of the limit contains that set. The conclusion is $\operatorname{mod}(U_n\setminus K_n)\to0$. Given an actual family, verify each hypothesis and identify this exact annulus before applying the theorem.

# Localization, protected carriers and marked widths

Here a *physical* assertion concerns specified sets, maps or curve families for a quadratic map. A fiber bijection, for example, must send the point represented by a lifted path to its actual endpoint. Matching the cardinalities of two fibers does not determine that bijection. The sections below identify the objects to construct, a separate way of checking the construction, and the conclusion that follows. “Written” refers to a mathematical argument in the archive. “Open” identifies the extra assertion needed for the indicated application. KKL refers to Kahn–Kapiamba–Lyubich, and WAD means weighted arc diagram. The four hypotheses in the tip preprint v2 are local to that preprint, distinct from the four global gates in `ResearchGates`. ## W1. Based sheets and a physical collision

**Paper 1, written for its stated seeds.** Fix connected based covers of the source and target surfaces, an embedding $a:X_s\hookrightarrow X_t$, and a based lift $A$ of $a\circ p_s$ to the target cover. Let $H_s,H_t$ be the images of the two based fundamental groups and put $K=a_*^{-1}H_t$. To construct the intermediate cover, take the component through the chosen lift of the basepoint in $X_s\times_{X_t}\widehat X_t$. Lifting a loop in this pullback gives the pair consisting of the loop and the lift of its image under $a$. Thus its endpoint is the basepoint exactly when the image loop belongs to $H_t$. The resulting component has subgroup $K$. Projection to the second factor has the continuous inverse $y\mapsto(a^{-1}(p_t y),y)$ on its image. This last formula requires the base embedding. [Pullback subgroup](../papers/paper1_canonical_localization/extracted/D_resolution/thm_00.tex) · [Embedded leg](../papers/paper1_canonical_localization/extracted/D_resolution/thm_01.tex)

To identify its based fiber in the source cover, lift representatives of $k\in K$ from the same basepoint. Following one path and reversing the other gives the loop-closure criterion for equality of their endpoints. With right monodromy this gives $H_s\backslash K$. Send $H_sk\mapsto k^{-1}H_s$ to use the Lean convention $u^{-1}v\in H_s$, retaining the order of multiplication. A based loop $g\in K\setminus H_s$ then has a source endpoint different from the base lift and the same endpoint as the base lift after $A$. To produce $g$ at the primitive seed, isolate an excess pullback component by a Jordan disk, join its oriented meridian to the fixed basepoint, compute its winding number about a point of the isolated excess component, and contract it in the filled target. The winding number is one for the positive meridian and zero on the source local subgroup, whose representatives miss the excess component in a simply connected local disk. The global degree count supplies an excess component, and the meridian argument distinguishes the two actual sheets. [Sheet theorem](../papers/paper1_canonical_localization/extracted/D_resolution/thm_02.tex) · [Excess component](../papers/paper1_canonical_localization/extracted/C_forced_collision/thm_05.tex) · [Based winding and contraction](../papers/paper1_canonical_localization/extracted/C_forced_collision/thm_06.tex) · [Lean coset criterion](weighted/LocalizationSheetKernel.lean)

Prove that both representatives stay in the fine exterior during the comparison. Record their successive transverse signed crossings and endpoint homotopies. Reduction of the resulting based word permits an independent subgroup-membership test. A shift of a rooted reference by $b_{00}^m$ changes the word to $g b_{00}^{-m}$ and preserves root membership. **A source-level proof of the rank substitution.** Start with a compact connected connector $C=B_0$ and pairwise disjoint full compact connected atoms $A_i$. Set $B=B_{i-1}$, $N=B\cup A_i$, $T=B\cap A_i=C\cap A_i\ne\varnothing$. Write $\sigma(X)$ for the number of components of the spherical complement minus one. Assume the finite component counts specified in Paper 3's attachment theorem. Work over the rationals, or tensor its integral sequences with $\mathbb Q$, which preserves exactness. Compact-pair Čech Mayer–Vietoris for $B,A_i$ gives the connecting and restriction maps. The initial reduced degree-zero groups vanish by connectedness. Alexander duality gives $\check H^1(A_i)=0$ by fullness and $\check H^2(N)=0$ since the spherical complement is nonempty. Exactness now yields

$$
 0\to\widetilde{\check H}^0(T)\to\check H^1(N)\to\ker r\to0,
 \qquad 0\to\ker r\to\check H^1(B)\xrightarrow{r}\check H^1(T)\to0.
$$

Here $r$ is restriction along the actual inclusion $T\subset B$. Duality identifies the degree-one ranks with $\sigma$, and the clopen decomposition of $T$ gives degree-zero reduced rank $c(T)-1$. Adding the rank equalities for the two sequences gives $\sigma(N)+\sigma(T)=\sigma(B)+c(T)-1$. For example, attach the closed disk to its boundary circle. The connected trace has $\sigma(T)=1$ and the bounded complementary component disappears. The Lean rank theorem verifies the last algebraic step. Instantiating the two actual Čech sequences in Lean remains open. [Attachment theorem and proof](../papers/paper3_protected_carriers/extracted/G_separation/separation_budget.tex) · [Lean rank identity](weighted/AttachmentDefectLedger.lean)

**Infinite horizon.** For fixed atoms $A_i$, independently exhibit decreasing nonempty full continua $C_n$ whose traces $C_n\cap A_i$ are nonempty and connected. Both intersections over $n$ are continua by nested compactness. The complement of $C_\infty$ is an increasing union of connected sets containing infinity. The physical full-connector criterion applied to the limiting traces gives fullness of $C_\infty\cup\bigcup_i A_i$. Retaining accesses additionally requires the paper's fixed obstacle and relative-collar clearance hypotheses. For a failure of a weaker limit rule, take the unit circles with shrinking open arcs removed. Each remaining closed arc is full, while its Hausdorff limit separates the plane. Selection of such decreasing envelopes for the dynamical towers remains a separate existence theorem. [Nested-limit stability and trace birth](../papers/paper3_protected_carriers/extracted/G_separation/separation_budget.tex)

**Dynamical cell-tree extraction, open.** For each virtual block and its prescribed horizon, construct the full connected carrier in $J_f$ containing the used little Julia sets and critical-orbit points, or account for each addition made by filling. Give an upper-semicontinuous decomposition into compact connected fibers. ## W5. From conformal charts to a bound for every Wave

Fix a selected tip $p\ge3$ and the actual domains $U_p\setminus T_p$, $V_p\setminus T'_p$. Define $P_p$ as the supremum for the target base $(I_9,I_0,I_1)$ and crest containing $I_0$. Define $C_p^+,C_p^-$ separately on the two prescribed source bases with crests $S_2,S_{18}$. These are suprema over families of proper leaves. Their definition presupposes the physical marking in W4. [v2 definition and `thm:gain`](../papers/tip_family_preprint_v2_20261116/sections/wave_proofs.tex)

1. **A lower bound needs a realizable family.** On the entire proposed rectangle or Schwarz–Christoffel quadrilateral verify conformal embedding, interior avoidance of the slit, simplicity, proper convergence to the assigned ideal ends, and the minimal base and crest of the foliation. For a vertical foliation in a rectangle of width $L$ and height $H$, length at least one on each leaf implies energy at least $L/H$ by integrating Cauchy–Schwarz across the rectangle. The density $1/H$ attains it. Conformal invariance transports this width and membership then bounds the physical supremum below. Radius inequalities alone check none of the endpoint or minimality claims.
2. **An upper bound needs all admissible leaves.** Prove each physical leaf in the indicated class has a lift in the designated all-path envelope. For a periodic cover with deck transformations, a nonnegative envelope metric gives the downstairs metric by square-summing the pullbacks over lifts. This density dominates the metric along each selected lift. Integration over a fundamental domain gives its energy by summing over translates. To subtract energy on an excluded box prove disjointness from **all required lifted curves** in the relevant construction, or independently prove that each leaf still has length at least one. Taking the infimum over admissible densities bounds each family and hence the supremum. An omitted winding can break the length bound without affecting any finite vertex test.
3. **The residual calculation is exact.** Reflection must carry the entire marked child class isometrically to its counterpart to write $C_p^+=C_p^-=C_p$. Given $P_p>L>0$ and $0<C_p<U$, inversion yields $D_p=2/C_p-1/P_p>2/U-1/L$. At $p=3$, taking $L=49/100,U=487/500$ gives $300/23863>1/100$. The other source cases are $p=4, 5\le p\le12, p\ge13$. The last range additionally uses a uniform analytic tail. These are conditional physical bounds under tip v2 hypotheses 1–3. [Source case bounds and analytic proof obligations](../papers/tip_family_preprint_v2_20261116/sections/wave_proofs.tex) · [Finite rational verifier](../research/independent_audits/tip_family_finite_p_wave_gain_20261116/verify.py)

For a claim about a **single** parent Wave, identify both proper child restrictions for every leaf and prove injectivity on their union. KKL Lemma A.11 then gives the harmonic upper bound $ab/(a+b)\le C_p/2$ for child widths $a,b\le C_p$. Optimizing only over these pairable parents gives $P_{p,2}\le C_p/2$, with the opposite weak sign in the reciprocal defect. This independently checks the quantifiers of the two conclusions. No optimizer or common pairing enters the independent-suprema calculation. [v2 `prop:loss`](../papers/tip_family_preprint_v2_20261116/sections/wave_proofs.tex)

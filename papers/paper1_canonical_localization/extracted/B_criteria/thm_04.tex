\begin{lemma}[One quadratic pullback of a full continuum]
\label{lem:quadratic-pullback-dichotomy}
Let \(q:U'\to U\) be a proper holomorphic map of degree two between
plane topological disks, let \(K\Subset U\) be a nonempty full
continuum, let \(c\) be the unique critical point of \(q\) and
\(v=q(c)\) its critical value.
\begin{enumerate}
\item If \(v\in K\), then \(q^{-1}K\) is a single full continuum, and
      the degree of \(q\) on it is two.
\item If \(v\notin K\), then \(q^{-1}K\) is the disjoint union of two
      full continua, each mapped homeomorphically onto \(K\).
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Choose a nested Jordan-domain basis \(V_r\) for \(K\) as in
Lemma~\ref{lem:jordan-basis}, with each \(\partial V_r\) avoiding
\(v\); if \(v\notin K\), choose in addition \(\overline V_1\) disjoint
from \(v\).

\emph{Case \(v\notin K\).}  Then \(v\notin V_r\) for every \(r\), so
\[
 q:q^{-1}(V_r)\longrightarrow V_r
\]
is an unbranched proper covering of total degree two.  Since \(V_r\)
is simply connected, its inverse image has exactly two components
\(W_r^0,W_r^1\), and both restrictions \(q:W_r^k\to V_r\) are
biholomorphisms.  The two inverse branches defined on \(V_1\) label
the components coherently across all \(r\), and
\[
 \overline W_{r+1}^k\subset W_r^k,\qquad k=0,1 .
\]
Set
\[
 L_k=\bigcap_{r\ge1}\overline W_r^k .
\]
Each \(L_k\) is nonempty, compact, and connected, being a nested
intersection of compact connected sets.  It is full because
\[
 \mathbb C\setminus L_k
 =
 \bigcup_{r\ge1}\bigl(\mathbb C\setminus\overline W_r^k\bigr)
\]
is an increasing union of connected sets, hence connected.  The inverse
branches identify each \(L_k\) homeomorphically with \(K\), and
\(q^{-1}K=L_0\sqcup L_1\).

\emph{Case \(v\in K\).}  Then every \(V_r\) contains \(v\).  The
component \(W_r\) of \(q^{-1}(V_r)\) containing the critical point
\(c\) already has degree at least two, so it has degree exactly two
and exhausts \(q^{-1}(V_r)\).  Riemann--Hurwitz for the degree-two
branched cover \(q:W_r\to V_r\) with one simple critical point gives
\[
 \chi(W_r)=2\chi(V_r)-1=1 .
\]
Thus \(W_r\) is a disk.  Because \(\partial V_r\) contains no critical
value, \(\partial W_r\) is a regular Jordan curve.  The compact Jordan
disks \(\overline W_r\) are nested, and
\[
 q^{-1}K=\bigcap_{r\ge1}\overline W_r .
\]
The same increasing-complement argument proves that this intersection
is connected and full, and the degree of \(q\) on it is two.

For completeness, the bordered Riemann--Hurwitz count can be obtained
by doubling \(W_r\) and \(V_r\) across their regular boundaries.  The
doubled degree-two map has the critical point and its mirror, so
\[
 2-2g=2\cdot2-2=2,
\]
whence \(g=0\); the double of a planar domain with \(b\) boundary
components has genus \(b-1\), so \(b=1\).
\end{proof}

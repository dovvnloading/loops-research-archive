\begin{definition}[Canonical localization data]\label{def:canonical-localization}
Let \(f:U^1\to U^0\) be an ordinary quadratic-like restriction of a quadratic
polynomial, primitively renormalizable of period \(p>1\). Put
\(F=f^p:U^p\to U^0\), \(A=K_p\), and \(B=\mathcal K_p=\bigcup_{j=0}^{p-1}f^j(K_p)\).
Kahn's canonical localization localizes the physical inclusion
\(i^p:T'\to T\), where
\[
T'=(U^p\setminus F^{-1}B)\cup A,\qquad T=(U^0\setminus B)\cup A,
\]
at the marked continuum \(A\): the source and target localization covers are the
connected covers of \(\Omega_s=U^p\setminus F^{-1}B\) and \(\Omega_t=U^0\setminus B\)
classified by the subgroups
\(H_s=\operatorname{im}[\pi_1(D_A\setminus A,z_*)\to\pi_1(\Omega_s,z_*)]\)
and its target analogue, where \(D_A\) is a small Jordan neighborhood of \(A\),
and the localized inclusion \(\hat i:T'_A\to T_A\) is the unique commuting based lift.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Pointed coset fiber]\label{def:pointed-coset}
For a based embedding \(a:(X_s,x_s)\hookrightarrow(X_t,x_t)\), localization
subgroups \(H_s,H_t\), and a based lift \(A\), put \(K=a_*^{-1}(H_t)\).
The pointed coset set \(K/H_s\) is the set of left cosets \(kH_s\), \(k\in K\),
with distinguished point \(H_s\). No group structure on \(K/H_s\) is used;
in particular \(H_s\) is not assumed normal in \(K\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Fixed direct-decoration pair and glued pseudo-piece]\label{def:direct-decoration}
The fixed Kahn--Lyubich direct-decoration pullback is
\(Q=Q_a\subset P=Y^{(\nu-1)q+1}\), \(g=f^q|_Q:Q\to P\), \(a\in\{L,R\}\),
with \(K_Q=Q\cap K(f)\), \(K_P=P\cap K(f)\), and the induced pair
\((g,\iota):Q\rightrightarrows P\) of the dynamical covering \(g\) and the
inclusion immersion \(\iota\) on the KL pseudo-pieces. The pseudo-piece is
glued from a central piece and universal-cover tiles \(\tilde A_{s,i}\) along
declared central/tile overlap embeddings \(e_i\) (source) and \(\tilde e_j\)
(target); \(L_i\) denotes the lifted tile immersion and \(j(i)\) the target
index of source tile \(i\). KL prove that \(\iota\) is a holomorphic immersion;
global injectivity at a general \(\psi\)-ql stage is a separate open condition.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[\(\psi\)-quadratic-like pair]\label{def:psi-ql}
A degree-two \(\psi\)-quadratic-like pair \((i,f):(U',K')\to(U,K)\) consists of
plane domains \(U',U\), full compact connected sets \(K'\subset U'\), \(K\subset U\),
a proper degree-two branched cover \(f:U'\to U\), and a holomorphic immersion
\(i:U'\to U\) with the exact common connected inverse
\(i^{-1}(K)=f^{-1}(K)=K'\) and \(i|_{K'}:K'\to K\) a homeomorphism.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Tile-injectivity glue criterion; manuscript Thm.~12.36, ledger \texttt{TILE\_INJECTIVITY\_GLUE\_EMBEDDING\_CRITERION}]\label{thm:glue-criterion}
For either fixed branch \(Q_a\), the map \(g\) is a biholomorphism and the inclusion component map is bijective. The inclusion immersion \(\iota:Q\to P\) is an interior holomorphic embedding if and only if every induced full universal-cover tile immersion \(L_i\) is injective. In that case the collision-relative overlap identity is
\[
L_i^{-1}\bigl(\tilde e_{j(i)}(Q_i)\bigr)=e_i(Q_i). \tag{10.34}
\]
Full inverse-image reflection over all of \(P_{j(i)}\) is not required.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The branch \(g:Q\to P\) is univalent and onto. Complete invariance of the filled Julia set gives \(g(K_Q)=K_P\), so \(g\) bijects the finite component sets of \(Q\setminus K_Q\) and \(P\setminus K_P\). The component map \(j\) induced by the inclusion is surjective by the pseudo-piece construction. Its finite domain and codomain have the same cardinality, hence \(j\) is bijective.

Let \(P_j\) be a target complementary component. Since \(Q\setminus K_Q=Q\cap(P\setminus K_P)\), partitioning both complements into components gives
\[
Q\cap P_j=\bigcup_{\{k:\,j(k)=j\}}Q_k .
\]
Bijectivity of \(j\) therefore gives \(Q\cap P_{j(i)}=Q_i\).

Assume every full tile map \(L_i\) is injective. In the quotient gluing, central--central and same-tile collisions are excluded. Distinct source tiles land in distinct target tiles because \(j\) is bijective, and KL's target tiles are disjoint outside their declared central overlaps.

It remains to consider a tile--central collision. Let \(x\) lie in source tile \(i\), let \(z\in Q\), and suppose their target quotient classes agree. The exact target tile--central intersection gives
\[
z\in Q\cap P_{j(i)}=Q_i,\qquad L_i(x)=\tilde e_{j(i)}(z).
\]
Overlap commutation gives \(L_i(e_i(z))=\tilde e_{j(i)}(z)=L_i(x)\). Tile injectivity forces \(x=e_i(z)\), so the two source representatives were already identified by the declared gluing. These four cases exhaust the quotient, proving injectivity of \(\iota\). Since KL already prove that it is a holomorphic immersion, it is an interior holomorphic embedding.

Conversely, every source tile embeds in \(Q\). If \(L_i\) had two distinct points with one image, their source quotient classes would be distinct while their \(\iota\)-images agreed. Thus glued injectivity implies tile injectivity. Finally, (10.34) follows directly: if \(L_i(x)=\tilde e_{j(i)}(q)\) with \(q\in Q_i\), commutation gives the same value at \(e_i(q)\), and tile injectivity gives \(x=e_i(q)\). The dynamical map has covering degree one and is therefore a biholomorphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Full abstract \(\psi\)-ql axioms do not imply global injectivity; manuscript Thm.~12.41, ledger \texttt{PSI\_QL\_GLOBAL\_INJECTIVITY\_NO\_GO}]\label{thm:psi-ql-no-go}
There is a degree-two \(\psi\)-quadratic-like pair
\[
(i,f):(U',K')\longrightarrow(U,K)
\]
for which \(f\) is a proper degree-two branched cover, \(i\) is a holomorphic immersion,
\(i^{-1}(K)=f^{-1}(K)=K'\)
is connected, and \(i|_{K'}:K'\to K\) is a homeomorphism, while the degree-one complementary-annulus immersion
\(i:U'\setminus K'\to U\setminus K\)
has a self-collision.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Put
\[
R=75,\qquad\beta=\frac{\pi}{50},\qquad\Delta=e^{2\beta}-1,
\]
and define
\[
U'=\{z:|z|<75\},\quad U=\{w:|w|<5625\},\quad f(z)=z^2,
\]
\[
K=[0,1],\qquad K'=[-1,1],\qquad i(z)=\frac{e^{\beta(z+1)}-1}{\Delta}. \tag{10.36}
\]
The intervals are compact, connected, full, and nondegenerate. The map \(f:U'\to U\) is a proper degree-two branched cover and
\[
f^{-1}([0,1])=[-1,1]. \tag{10.37}
\]
Moreover, \(i'(z)=\beta e^{\beta(z+1)}/\Delta\neq 0\). For \(|z|<75\), the elementary inequalities \(3<\pi<\tfrac{22}{7}\), \(e<3\), \(e^{x}>1+x\) \((x>0)\) give
\[
|i(z)|<\frac{e^{76\beta}+1}{e^{2\beta}-1}<\frac{3^{5}+1}{3/25}=\frac{6100}{3}<5625. \tag{10.38}
\]
Here \(76\beta=38\pi/25<836/175<5\); the strict bound \(\pi<22/7\) follows, for example, from
\[
\frac{22}{7}-\pi=\int_0^1\frac{x^4(1-x)^4}{1+x^2}\,dx>0,
\]
and \(e<3\) follows from its factorial series. Thus \(i\) maps the source disk into the target disk.

For \(t\in[0,1]\),
\[
i(z)=t\iff z=-1+\frac{\log(1+\Delta t)}{\beta}+100ik,\quad k\in\mathbb Z. \tag{10.39}
\]
The real part in (10.39) belongs to \([-1,1]\), while every nonzero sheet has imaginary modulus at least \(100>75\). Consequently
\[
i^{-1}(K)=[-1,1]=f^{-1}(K).
\]
On this interval \(i\) is strictly increasing and sends \(-1\mapsto 0\), \(1\mapsto 1\).

The circle \(|z|=2\) is a positive source-annulus generator. The equation \(i(z)=1/2\) has exactly one simple solution inside that circle: its solutions have the form
\[
-1+\beta^{-1}\log(1+\Delta/2)+100ik,
\]
and the \(k=0\) real part lies strictly between \(-1\) and \(1\). The argument principle therefore gives complementary-annulus degree \(+1\).

Nevertheless, \(z_+=50i\) and \(z_-=-50i\) are distinct points of \(U'\setminus K'\), and
\[
\beta(z_+-z_-)=2\pi i,\qquad i(z_+)=i(z_-)=-\frac{e^{\beta}+1}{\Delta}<0 .
\]
Their common image belongs to \(U\setminus K\), proving the collision.
\end{proof}

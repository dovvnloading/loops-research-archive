\begin{theorem}[Infinite canonical sheet defect; manuscript Thm.~12.51]\label{thm:infinite-sheet-defect}
At every ordinary primitive canonical seed,
\[
[K:H_s]=\infty .
\]
Hence \(q\) is infinite-sheeted and every fiber
\[
A^{-1}(e(z))=q^{-1}(z),\qquad z\in P_K,
\]
is infinite.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let \(x\) lie in the excess component and let \(\gamma\) be the peripheral class in the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:forced-collision}. Winding about \(x\) is a homomorphism \(w:\pi_1(\Omega_s)\to\mathbb Z\) with
\[
w(H_s)=0,\qquad w(\gamma)=1,\qquad \gamma\in K .
\]
If \(\gamma^{m}H_s=\gamma^{n}H_s\), then \(\gamma^{m-n}\in H_s\), so winding gives \(m-n=0\). Thus the cosets \(\gamma^{n}H_s\), \(n\in\mathbb Z\), are pairwise distinct. The fiber conclusion follows from Theorem~\ref{thm:sheet-resolution}.
\end{proof}

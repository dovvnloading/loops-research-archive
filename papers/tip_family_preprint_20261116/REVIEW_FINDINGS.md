## Corrected error and material gaps

**RF-01 — CONFIRMED ERROR IN HISTORICAL AUDIT (corrected here).** The merged real_primitive_tip_family_uniform_20261116/NOTE.md contains the obsolete claim that the three selected crossing/avoiding shortest source arcs all span 3 atoms. The avoiding pair (S3,S17) instead has the inclusive short arc S17,S18,S19,S0,S1,S2,S3 of **7**, while its image (I3,I7) has span **5**. The accurate source lengths are **3,3,7**, and cover-pushed target lengths are **3,3,5**. The later unconditional audit and tip_family_theorem/AUDIT.md also document this correction. The new paper gives the full arc and does not inherit the false sentence. Every selected class still passes KKL Appendix A.1's strict greater-than-two filter.

**RF-02 — CHRONOLOGY / OVERCLAIM RISK.** An older theorem audit regards strict canonical gain as open or conditional. Later tip_family_finite_p_wave_gain and tip_family_hypothesis_M_discharge audits establish a narrower, **independently optimized unbuffered** theorem on the selected physical tree-slit surfaces. The present paper states that later result and does not extrapolate it to one parent and its children, a different puzzle, or the literal buffered WAD.

**RF-03 — EXTERNAL-THEOREM TRUST BOUNDARY.** The Python and Lean checks prove integer/rational and finite incidence predicates, **not** Douady–Hubbard straightening, the Misiurewicz dendrite theorem, McMullen almost-disjointness, Carathéodory prime-end extension, KKL immersion and restriction semantics, or conformal SC extremal width. The paper writes their hypotheses and applications and cites the named external results. Those hand-written continuum steps still require independent expert review; no computation substitutes for them.

**RF-04 — KKL REFERENCE STATUS.** Kahn–Kapiamba–Lyubich, arXiv:2606.27272v1 (2026), is a publicly posted research preprint, not known to be a peer-reviewed journal article. The paper cites it by authors, paper, numbered Propositions 6.5–6.6 and Lemma A.11, relevant sections and Appendix A, without calling it a reviewed publication.

**RF-05 — MARKING IDENTITY NECESSARY.** Equal finite atom tables or homeomorphic Hubbard trees do **not** imply equal extremal widths. The later M-discharge proves the point-set and conformal identity of the actual selected U_p minus T_p and V_p minus T'_p with their slit models. The paper includes the necessary marked-closure and density-change argument. Width transport to an alternative ambient/puzzle surface is NOT proved.

**RF-06 — LEAFWISE VERSUS INDEPENDENT GAIN.** KKL Lemma A.11 gives the **opposite** sign for a single parent whose every leaf has two proper, injectively paired children. The paper proves P_pair <= C/2 and 2/C - 1/P_pair <= 0, then separately proves 2/C - 1/P > 1/100 for unrestricted independent suprema. An inference of positive gain from the same parent/child pair would be a gap and is explicitly rejected.

**RF-07 — BUFFERED WAD REMAINS OPEN.** The four-hole Y_K surface and buffered coefficients (q_gamma-2)_+ are not the one-slit unbuffered A.1 optimization. Only outer-incident endpoint types for periods >=11 are excluded. Six inner endpoint types, potentially infinitely many classes within each, have unknown classwise q_gamma and possibly no positive support. The full WAD cannot be declared positive, zero or amplifying from the current audits.

**RF-08 — DEPTH UNIFORMITY NOT PROVED.** Nine depth-two charts and one depth-three (3,3,3) chart are exact finite results, not all-depth statements. Local straightening and optimal real complex bounds do not identify entire prime-end-marked slit surfaces with depth-independent QC constant. The paper gives a strictly conditional two-sided cofinal-width theorem and an annulus countermodel to the invalid local-to-global inference.

**RF-09 — CREST LIMIT IS CONJECTURAL.** The finite selected four-push full-circular collar data cover periods 3..16, and the older fixed-bin data describe **different, smaller collars**. In particular the period-three fixed-bin ratio ~0.568 is not the full-collar ~0.980. The numerical c_infty ~0.347005 and proposed O(4^-p) error require uniform complex-domain C1 control, gate transversality, proper-path survival and width estimates absent from the finite certificate. The paper labels them conjectural.

**RF-10 — CORRECT PARAMETER AND ROUCHE SCOPE.** For 3..12 uniqueness is only within the ten exact seed charts; for >=13 it is only within the explicit kneading window. The p-return degree-two central restriction uses R3=1/4 or Rp=2^-p and full-disk margin >1/25, while the distinct normalized third-return uses disk 3/10 and gap >1/8. There is no interpolation across the p=12/13 seam or global real-root classification. The original polynomial has degree 2^p, not 2.

**RF-11 — REFERENCE-SPECIFICITY CORRECTION.** The archival theorem audit records an older mistaken Rouché locator. Use Ahlfors, Complex Analysis, 3rd ed., Ch. 4 section 5.2, corollary to Theorem 18; Forster section 17.14 for Riemann–Hurwitz; Douady–Hubbard 1985 Proposition 2/Theorem 1; McMullen 1994 Theorem 7.3; and KKL 2026 as a preprint.

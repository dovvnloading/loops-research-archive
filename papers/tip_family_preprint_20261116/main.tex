\begin{abstract}
For every integer $p\ge3$ we construct a selected real primitive-tip parameter $c_p$ with $c_p+2\sim(3\pi^2/2)4^{-p}$, proper degree-two first and third polynomial-like returns, and uniform Rouch\'e margins.  Straightening, dendrite, prime-end and Kahn--Kapiamba--Lyubich (KKL) theorems identify the physical period-three little Julia intervals, ten/twenty ideal atoms, one attachment, distinct covering and immersion, the golden-ratio edge matrix and zero-old-edge crests.  On the physical marked slit surfaces, a radial lower Wave and a lifted-quadrilateral upper envelope give a canonical marked-width sandwich.  Embedded parent quadrilaterals and domain-sensitive source metrics imply the strict \emph{independent unbuffered} defect $D_p=2/C_p-1/P_p>1/100$ for all $p\ge3$, where parent and children are optimized separately; the leafwise-pairable parent obeys KKL's opposite loss-or-equality inequality.  For the \emph{different} buffered canonical WAD we exclude every outer-incident class for $p\ge11$ but leave its inner support open.  Finite crest and nested-renormalization certificates are distinguished from conjectural limits and missing depth-uniform global marked quasiconformal transport.  No Mandelbrot-set local-connectivity result is claimed.  This unreviewed manuscript was prepared with AI assistance from the versioned archive.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and proof status}
This paper studies a \emph{real}, selected, postcritically finite quadratic family.  The period-three tip belongs to the $p$-return, not to the original polynomial of period $p$.  In particular $x_{jp}=f^{jp}(0)$, not $x_j=f^j(0)$.  The results concern the physical tree slit and its central degree-two pullback, not an arbitrary puzzle or a global Mandelbrot statement.  KKL \cite{KKL2026} is a posted research preprint, not a claimed peer-reviewed journal publication.

Each proof distinguishes exact rational interval computations, written complex-analytic deductions, and named external theorems.  Passing a Python or Lean check certifies the finite premises it implements but never formalizes KKL, straightening, Carath\'eodory boundaries, or conformal extremal width.

# Problem 4.3: ordinary satellite modulus obstruction / pseudo-Siegel test boundary

**Claim (frozen pre-registration; clarified scope by review):** For actual nested satellite polynomial-like (PL) cycles satisfying BLOT Definitions 2.3–2.4 at each level, with return degree two and unbounded relative periods, no choice of ordinary fundamental annuli can have a uniform positive lower modulus bound. The chains below are symbolic examples of the published bound, not realized or measured cycles. The quantitative test below evaluates the published universal upper envelope, not a computed conformal modulus of an individual parameter. The proposed use of this observable as a direct test of pseudo-Siegel a priori bounds is ill-posed without a comparison theorem.

**Final research label: imported + bounded negative result.** The obstruction is a published theorem (Blokh–Levin–Oversteegen–Timorin, already explicitly applied to infinitely renormalizable quadratics on p. 2), not a new theorem or new dynamical result. The finite arithmetic certifies only evaluations of that upper envelope on symbolic examples; the bounded negative result is the refusal of the proposed proxy because its comparison is unproved. Problem 4.3 is unchanged by this work and remains open in the cited survey; no proof or disproof of it or MLC is offered. No individual parameter, pseudo-Siegel disk, or little-copy diameter will be inferred from a rotation denominator. No other archive obligation or ledger status is changed. This is a standalone source/type audit of the survey, not a model-to-geometry bridge for H1–H4.

### Literature and observable

- Blokh, Levin, Oversteegen, Timorin, *Maps with No a Priori Bounds*, CMP 406:141 (2025), DOI [10.1007/s00220-025-05303-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-025-05303-8), Main Theorem, printed/PDF p. 2 of 15, Definitions 2.3–2.4 p. 6 and §2.2 proof p. 8 ([version-of-record PDF](https://publications.hse.ru/pubs/share/direct/1052158150.pdf)). Let f be a rational map of degree at least two, with q distinct PL sets from a connected filled PL Julia set K* for f^q:U→V, Jordan domains U compactly contained in V. The cycle must have r<q components around a repelling r-cycle B, each consisting of s=q/r≥2 members sharing the corresponding point of B. Intersections between distinct members are at most one point of B; K*∖B must be connected for every member (otherwise the paper distinguishes cross renormalization). For any admissible degree-d* PL representative of any member, the theorem bounds the fundamental modulus by (d*−1) times the root modulus, strictly below d*(d*−1)π/ln(4(s+1)). Specialize to d*=2 (also the maximum allowed by Lemma 2.6 for a quadratic polynomial). Writing m=mod(V∖closure(U)), the ordinary conformal modulus in the source's units (consistent with the Teichmüller estimate on p. 4, τ(m)>16 exp(−π/(2m)); no reciprocal or 2π rescaling), we obtain
  m ≤ mod(U∖K*) < B(s):=2π/log(4(s+1)).
  This is an upper bound for every admissible ordinary PL representative of the specified cycle, not a formula or lower bound for m. The paper itself draws the no-bounds implication for unbounded satellite relative periods on pp. 1–2. The calculation does not verify a specific quadratic parameter or any of the geometric hypotheses.
- Dudko–Lyubich, *Uniform a priori bounds for neutral renormalization*, [arXiv:2210.09280v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.09280v2). The authors' abstract concerns uniform pseudo-Siegel control for bounded-type Siegel disks, a Mother Hedgehog for neutral quadratic dynamics, and sector renormalization; it supplies no comparison to an ordinary satellite PL annulus in this packet. The committed CI record locates Theorems 1.1–1.3 on PDF pp. 3–4; independent theorem-text verification of this exact PDF version is pending, so no detailed theorem is invoked. The survey's Theorem 4.2 and (4.7), checked in versioned HTML §4.4, describe almost-invariant disks, quasiconformal control after sector renormalization, and fjord regularization. The remaining satellite extension is the target of Problem 4.3.
- Kahn–Lyubich, *A priori bounds for some infinitely renormalizable quadratics: IV. Elephant Eyes*, [arXiv:2601.21905v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.21905v1), §4.0.1 (E1–E3: rotation 1/q, only K0 in the central domain, p=q+b with bounded b), §7.1–7.2 (Theorem 7.1, amplification in the specified pseudo-ql setting), and §8 (unbranched bounds); CI marker pages 9, 16 and 18, respectively. Large q and p=q+O(1) alone do not establish these hypotheses. The unbranched bounds of §8 concern fundamental annuli avoiding noncentral little Julia sets, which cannot be inferred for a pure satellite cycle with touching pieces: another attached connected piece extending outside the outer domain would have to cross the collar. No Elephant Eyes result is applied to our examples.

## Frozen quantitative pre-registration

The original pre-registration is preserved at commit `58d58db061a8c1bf005fdf7b024b8c93e4749b2b`. Review clarifications below do not change the sampling, threshold or arithmetic rule. Define a symbolic satellite tuning chain by successive rational relative rotations r_k=a_k/s_k, gcd(a_k,s_k)=1, k≥2, and periods P_1=1, P_k=P_(k-1)*s_k. Use the conventional satellite tuning combinatorics; conclusions are conditional on actual realization with the stated PL cycle at the stage, not existence/uniqueness of a limiting parameter. If such a chain is realized by one quadratic f with genuine nested satellite PL cycles, at level k the global cycle has q=P_k distinct connected filled PL sets; the parent attachment orbit must be repelling of period r=P_(k−1), its r components have s_k sets at their non-separating attachment points, and there must be no other intersections. Under exactly these hypotheses BLOT's relative period is s_k=P_k/P_(k−1), and its degree-two bound applies to f^(P_k) directly. The global polynomial degree stays two. No construction, orbit or geometric verification of any such cycle is supplied by the rational rotations alone, including the Fibonacci and constant examples. Do not transfer a modulus through a parent straightening across an unverified hybrid collar.

Four chains (fixed before arithmetic):
- linear: r_k=1/k, s_k=k;
- exponential: r_k=1/2^k;
- constant control: r_k=1/2;
- golden convergent comparison: r_k=F_k/F_(k+1), F_1=F_2=1. Although the continued-fraction digits are bounded, these are distinct rational relative rotations at successive satellite levels, so this is **not** a bounded-satellite-relative-period control. It is a second unbounded-denominator diagnostic, not the single neutral golden-mean map.

Sample all chains at k=2,...,32,64,128; additionally linear at 256,1024,1048576. Use exact positive rationals and certified intervals for B only. The finite falsifiable surrogate H(delta): every admissible ordinary PL modulus at the registered stages is ≥delta=1/2 (chosen diagnostic threshold, not a constant from DL). Reject H on a chain if any certified upper endpoint of B is <1/2; otherwise report inconclusive, never pass. This is a necessary-condition test, not measurement of actual moduli. Preregister prediction: linear at k=1048576 and exponential at k=32 reject; golden at k=32 rejects; constant B stays identical and the test is inconclusive. No parameter-plane or orbit computations are planned. Analytic asymptotic test: B(k)=O(1/log k), B(2^k)=O(1/k), B(F_(k+1))=O(1/k); any uniform m>0 is impossible along these realized unbounded chains, and this assertion follows from the imported theorem irrespective of the finite test. For a realized bounded-type ql chain the survey's Thm 4.1 (§4.3) separately asserts a priori bounds; this does not validate the universal quantifier in H or verify the symbolic constant chain's realization.

Error protocol: compute ln via binary range reduction and atanh rational series with an explicit positive tail, π via Machin and alternating arctan bounds, 80 terms each, outward decimal endpoints to 40 places, require displayed width ≤ 2×10^-40; assert exact rational comparisons, monotonicity and negative controls. No floating point, randomness or fitted rate. Only B's interval is narrow; the certified enclosure for m itself is (0,B_upper), not [B_lower,B_upper]. If the certificate fails, report failure rather than relax the criteria.

Hostile self-check: the annulus U∖K* and the fundamental annulus are not equal by definition; take the exact stated inequality, do not equate B with either modulus. Filled pseudo-Siegel fjords and sector quotients change the domain and admissible curve family. Neither the comparison of their widths with m nor a comparison between consecutive copy diameters and m is supplied by any cited theorem. A uniform quasidisk does not specify an exterior collar with definite annulus modulus; even concentric round disks permit arbitrarily thin collars. If a future construction on such cycles simultaneously establishes a uniform bound M_k≥ε>0 for a specified pseudo-Siegel modulus and m_k≥c M_k for fixed c>0, these two assertions would contradict the imported obstruction along an unbounded-period subsequence. Merely having M_k>0 does not suffice; the argument neither falsifies a comparison in isolation nor the pseudo-Siegel conjecture. No such bridge is assumed. This is the bounded negative alternative to a direct Problem 4.3 test.

### Soundness of arithmetic and hostile post-check

For integer x≥2 put e=floor(log2 x) by bit length and y=x/2^e. Then ln x=e ln 2+ln y. For 1≤y≤2, t=(y−1)/(y+1)∈[0,1/3]; the first N=80 terms of 2 sum_{j≥0} t^(2j+1)/(2j+1) are a lower bound, and the omitted positive tail is at most 2t^(2N+1)/((2N+1)(1−t²)). This also bounds ln 2. The alternating series for atan(1/5), atan(1/239) is enclosed by consecutive partial sums; π=16 atan(1/5)−4 atan(1/239). Positive division gives [2π_lower/ln_upper, 2π_upper/ln_lower]. Integer floor/ceiling provide outward endpoints. Each width and the comparisons are asserted exactly; the verifier also checks coprimality, monotonic disjointness of successive nonconstant B intervals, constant equality, and coarse π, ln 2 and B(2) sanity bounds. The latter check the implemented expression, not the geometric modulus normalization, which is taken directly from the published theorem. No numerical linearization, finite orbit truncation or floating-point estimates enter.

A priori bounds normally quantify existence of *some* good domains at each scale, not that every arbitrarily chosen restriction has a good collar. The literal frozen H above is stronger than the usual existence hypothesis. This matters for the control: a thin collar may defeat H even when good collars exist. Crucially, BLOT bounds **every** admissible ordinary restriction, so a certified B<δ rejects even the weaker, meaningful existence hypothesis (there exists a collar of modulus ≥δ at each registered level). No post hoc threshold change is used.

For a realized infinite satellite chain with the period and full Definition 2.4 hypotheses above at each level, the BLOT bound holds at every k, so the supremum of the ordinary fundamental annulus moduli over all admissible restrictions is at most B(s_k). If s_k→∞ this supremum tends to zero. For linear s=k, B∼2π/ln k; exponential s=2^k gives B∼(2π/ln 2)/k; Fibonacci denominators yield B∼(2π/ln φ)/k with φ=(1+√5)/2. These are asymptotics of the envelope; the actual moduli may vanish much faster. For unbounded but not divergent s_k, a subsequence suffices to rule out a positive uniform bound. No realization claim is made: the pointwise implication applies whenever the hypothesized cycles exist, while the assertion about a single infinite chain is conditional on its realization. No infinite tuning limit or uniqueness is certified.

### Why this fails as a direct P4.3 experiment; next admissible observable

The survey's neutral construction uses almost-invariant pseudo-Siegel disks and fjord filling to absorb degeneration; this changes the geometry relative to a genuine small Julia-set PL cycle. The packet does not construct such a filling at a satellite level. A genuine fundamental annulus must separate a compact inner domain from its outer boundary in the original plane; a sector renormalization or filled-fjord regularization may have different boundaries, quotient identifications and admissible paths. No conformal equivalence, monotonic comparison in the needed direction, or uniform distortion constant is provided. The theorem-based degeneration therefore supplies no logical contradiction to the proposed bounded regularized geometry. It is not evidence either for or against it. To make a follow-up falsifiable, first specify the source-defined pseudo-Siegel disks/sector charts, dams, first-return horizon and curve family at each actual complex satellite level, then certify either their normalized external interval extremal widths (uniform upper test) or a source-defined collar modulus (uniform lower test); certify the physical identification and any transport before numerical comparison. Denominator-only tuning codes cannot select these domains. The constant control is not a measurement, and the golden convergent comparison is not a bounded-relative-period control.

**Not established:** actual annulus measurements or exact modulus values; a sharp decay rate for m; any pseudo-Siegel bound or counterexample; existence/uniqueness of a limit of the prescribed tuning chains; actual copy diameters or their ratios; domain construction or a regularized-to-ordinary modulus bridge; Problem 4.4, full MLC, any ledger gate or a new imported theorem. The sole dynamical obstruction used here is the published Main Theorem, applied conditionally with its stated hypotheses. The numerical component is a finite certificate of its bound, not independent empirical evidence of new dynamics.

# Quadratic critical-relation transversality (Lean development)

Target: the classical transversality input in [MISIUREWICZ.md §2](../sharp_boundary_frontier_20261126/MISIUREWICZ.md), a local finite-orbit input related to H4_RIGIDITY. No global gate is promoted. This is formalization of classical complex dynamics, not a priority claim for the mathematical transversality theorem. See [Hutz–Towsley, *Misiurewicz points for polynomial maps and transversality* (2015), §3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.4048) for the algebraic-number approach.

## Statements and proof

Let P_0(c)=0, P_(j+1)(c)=P_j(c)^2+c, and D_j(c)=P'_j(c). The Lean module proves the actual complex derivative recurrence D_0=0, D_(j+1)=2P_j D_j+1. Integer orbit polynomials P_j (j>0) are monic of degree 2^(j-1). Thus every parameter with P_a=P_b for a<b is an algebraic integer, by the monic difference polynomial. All orbit values and tangent values are integral there.

* `center_transverse`: at every positive critical return P_n=0, the derivative D_n is nonzero. Otherwise 2(-P_(n-1)D_(n-1))=1 in the algebraic integers, impossible since 1/2 is not integral over Z.
* `first_collision_deriv_ne_zero`: at P_(a+2)=P_(b+2), a<b, with P_(a+1)≠P_(b+1), the actual complex derivative of P_(b+2)-P_(a+2) is nonzero. This is the first-collision transversality statement, without a repelling-multiplier premise.
* `signed_relation_unit_mod_two` and `first_collision_deriv_factor_unit` refine the proof by providing explicit arithmetic witnesses (verification of these two added declarations requires the new CI run).

For the signed relation P_(a+1)+P_(b+1)=0, set A=P_a, B=P_b, S=A D_a+B D_b. The derivative recurrence gives S=A+B+2t for an integral t, including the time-zero case. The signed relation gives (A+B)^2=2(AB-c), hence S^2=2z where z=AB-c+2(A+B)t+2t^2 is integral. Set q=1+S, r=1-S, k=-z. Then D_(a+1)+D_(b+1)=2q and qr=1+2k. In particular q is a unit modulo the ideal generated by 2 in the algebraic integers; its valuation is zero at every prime over two. The Lean statement is the explicit witness identity, not a formalization of local valuations. At an unequal-predecessor collision, factoring the difference of squares gives P_(a+1)=-P_(b+1), and the derivative difference is exactly 4 P_(b+1) q. The predecessor is nonzero. This is stronger than a bare nonvanishing statement and identifies the arithmetic factor. No uniform complex-modulus lower bound follows merely from this congruence.

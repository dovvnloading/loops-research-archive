## 2. Exact target trichotomy

**Theorem 2.1 (complete strict finite J-side target/extension/properness trichotomy).** Fix **any** original tile \(j\in\{0,1,2,3\}\) and **any** finite ideal J-side interior point \((j,x)\in\mathcal F_j^{J,\mathrm{strict}}\). Let \(\theta=x\bmod1\), \(n=x-\theta\in\mathbb Z\), and let \(q=L_G(\theta)\in K_3\) be the physical landing of the corresponding G ray. Exactly one of the following occurs.

**Case R — physical marked root \(q\in K_9\).** The genuine W-sector theorem of merged PR #64 puts \(\theta\) in exactly one \(E_i=\{\vartheta\in I_i:L_G(\vartheta)\in K_9\}\). Define the original same-V-parent mate \(m(0)=1,m(1)=0,m(2)=3,m(3)=2\). The fine ray lift is **root-convergent and hence improper in the filled fine surface** if and only if

\[
\boxed{j=m(i),\quad n=0.}\tag{4}
\]

Every other *strict* J-side lift of a ray physically landing on \(K_9\) is a **proper nonroot end** in the uncompactified \(\widehat T_9\), with no continuous extension there. The own-seam central root representative \(j=i,n=0\) is not a member of (3); it belongs to the **glued open seam**, not a KL J-side.

**Case X — physically omitted phase \(q\in D_{0,1}\cup D_{0,2}\).** Because \(q\notin T_9\), **no** projection-commuting lift can continuously extend to a point of \(\widehat T_9\) at this finite original J-side point, irrespective of its tile, free-group sheet or integer winding. Every such lift is proper in \(\widehat T_9\) as it approaches its ideal end.

**Case O — ordinary fine exterior \(q\in K_3\setminus(K_9\cup D_{0,1}\cup D_{0,2})\).** The projected landing lies in \(\Omega_9\). Every specified interior fine lift (including all its source-unwrapped integer translations) converges to a **unique ordinary interior point of** \(\widehat\Omega_9\) above \(q\). The lift therefore **does extend locally and uniquely** at that finite source ideal point and is **improper** in the full fine surface. Different source sheets or side points are **not asserted to have equal limit points**.

The three cases are mutually exclusive and exhaustive for every **strict interior finite** KL J-side point, since \(L_G\) is defined continuously on the G external angle circle and its image is \(K_3\).

### Proof

The source-dependence is explicit. By the real-symmetric postcritically finite/subhyperbolic G straightening, the G external Carathéodory landing \(L_G\) is a continuous surjection \(S^1\to K_3\). Its source-sector labels and actual universal-cover angle-coordinate prime ends are provided by the original KL path-space gluing and the source-certified W side atlas; these are **not** physical ambient f-ray labels.

For (R), apply merged PR #64's *parameterized* source result, not its eight-ray finite regression. It used the **literal KL W→V universal-tile inclusion naturality** to prove the positive mate zero-shift section and the genuine two-real-wake transverse character to rule out all opposite-parent shifts, then merged PR #63's full coarse-meridian winding obstruction to reject same-parent nonzero shifts. The associated Kahn biholomorphic root chart proves that every nonroot tail with physical endpoint in the *unique* marked \(K_9\) has **no** accumulation point anywhere in the full uncompactified fine target. Hence (4) holds **as a statement about source-side points**, not an unrelated selected physical-ray lift.

For (X), let \(\gamma(t)\) be an interior source tile arc approaching the specified finite J-side prime end, with \(p_W(\gamma(t))\to q\in D_{0,1}\cup D_{0,2}\), as given by the actual source G ray landing and KL universal-cover boundary. If \(j_W(\gamma(t_k))\) had any compact accumulation in \(\widehat T_9\), continuity of \(\pi_9\) would put \(q\) in the *image* \(T_9\), contradiction. Thus every lift **escapes every compact set** and cannot have a continuous extension into \(\widehat T_9\). This logic does not involve any chosen based coset or single ray sample.

For (O), choose a simply connected disk \(B\Subset\Omega_9\) around \(q\). The projection \(\pi_9:\widehat\Omega_9\to\Omega_9\) is an unramified topological/holomorphic covering. The genuine G Carathéodory landing map ensures that, for a sufficiently small finite source ideal-side prime-end collar at \((j,x)\), its *entire* physical image lies in \(B\). Since this collar is connected, the interior image under \(j_W\) lies within a single connected evenly covered component of \(\pi_9^{-1}(B)\). The inverse local covering chart there extends \(j_W\) continuously to the finite ideal point and across a suitably small neighboring ideal-side interval, with limit the unique point of that component over \(q\). This proves both existence and uniqueness of the **local continuation of the given interior lift**; it does not equate two lifts with distinct starting sheets.

Finally, (1) exhausts every possible physical landing \(q\in K_3\). All three categories are therefore complete on (3). \(\square\)

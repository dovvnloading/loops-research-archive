Date: 2026-07-25

Principal result
----------------
For one fixed Kahn-Lyubich direct-decoration block and the actual binary
f^q pullback package Q_L,Q_R, the source depth-n attached-pullback theorem
recovers the missing first-level contact vertices. Exact boundary-word matching
then recovers the open ray contacts and corner flags. The two one-step contact
graphs generate every all-depth binary contact through an exact normalized
recursion. At source depth n all words synchronize to the fixed alpha port; L
preserves it and R kills it, so the post-transient contact system has two
states. A one-to-many pseudo-puzzle trace atlas, endpoint-germ itineraries,
normalized-overlay naturality, safe-gap enlargement invariance, and certified
finite-refinement naturality are proved in this scope.

The exact binary response has a shared recursive specification of size O(r)
at depth r. Separately, any binary response/WAD/Value observable generated by
a d-state coefficient transducer and w-dimensional branch transport has an
exact future quotient of dimension at most d*w. For one source-licensed finite
terminal Hubbard tree, the actual KKL Value formula further gives an exact
positive second-moment state of dimension at most |E|^2(|E|^2+1)/2 and a
completely positive binary branch transport. Universal terminal-tree dimension
and the virtual typed factor remain open.

Not claimed
-----------
No universal bound in q, n, Wave horizon, or virtual depth is claimed. The work
does not construct the global source-complete Wave inventory, prove that KKL
Value/WAD data satisfy a universal finite-state transducer law, construct a
universal future-observable response/WAD/Value quotient, prove MBTT,
coercivity, rigidity, UPTCR, or MLC.

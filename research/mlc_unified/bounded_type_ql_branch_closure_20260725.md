# Bounded-type quadratic-like branch closure certificate

Date: 2026-07-25  
Status: imported from the current primary survey; scope is exact.

## Closed statement

For every infinitely renormalizable quadratic whose quadratic-like
renormalization combinatorics are of bounded type, a priori bounds and MLC
hold at the corresponding parameter. The corresponding Julia sets are
covered by the same theorem package.

## Source verification

Dudko, *On the MLC Conjecture and the Renormalization Theory in Complex
Dynamics*, arXiv:2512.24171, Theorem 4.1, states that a priori bounds and MLC
hold for all ql-combinatorics of bounded type and explicitly includes the
period-doubling Feigenbaum parameter.

The relevant source passage is the theorem statement immediately following
the bounded-type satellite analysis in Section 4.3. This is an imported
scope result, not a claim derived from the local virtual-cylinder files.

## Consequence for the actual global proof

The global attack can remove both:

1. the finite-decoration branch, closed separately by the
   Kahn--Lyubich source certificate; and
2. the bounded-type ql branch, closed by Dudko's Theorem 4.1.

The unresolved target is now the genuinely unbounded satellite ql case and
the virtual near-Molecule interpolation between primitive and satellite
scales. The same survey labels these as Problems 4.3 and 4.4; no claim of
global MLC follows until those cases are supplied.

# Finite primitive-root parabolic anchor family

## Exact construction

For a primitive (q)-th root (lambda), use

\[
f_c(z)=z^2+c,\qquad z_\alpha=\lambda/2,
\qquad c=\lambda/2-\lambda^2/4.
\]

In the local coordinate (w=z-z_\alpha), the germ is exactly

\[
g(w)=\lambda w+w^2.
\]

The validator computes (g^q) modulo (w^{q+2}) in the exact cyclotomic
ring (mathbb Q[x]/(\Phi_q(x))). For every order (q=2,\ldots,16), it
checks:

1. (\Phi_q(\lambda)=0) and (\lambda^q=1);
2. (z_\alpha) is a fixed point of (f_c) with multiplier (\lambda);
3. the coefficient of (w) in (g^q) is (1);
4. coefficients of (w^2,\ldots,w^q) vanish exactly; and
5. the coefficient of (w^{q+1}) is nonzero exactly.

The output is `parabolic_root_family_20260726.json`, generated by
`validate_parabolic_root_family.py`. All checks pass.

## Scope boundary

This is a finite exact local certificate. Together with the all-q
critical-point basin-count argument in
`parabolic_resonance_recurrence_20260725.md`, it supports the correction that
primitive rational satellite roots are genuinely nondegenerate
parabolic/cylindrical anchors. It does **not** prove uniform cylinder
estimates, Ecalle-phase transport, realization of an arbitrary infinite
satellite tower, Molecule hyperbolicity, or MLC.

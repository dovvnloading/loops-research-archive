# Research Push II: adversarial assessment and integration record

## 1. Verdict

Research Push II contains a useful cluster of correct local theorems and
counterexamples.  It does **not** close the current virtual construction
gate, and its proposed promotions of `MAPPING_TELESCOPE` and
`FINITE_HORIZON_ASSEMBLY` are not justified.

The material adopted into the project is the narrowed, independently
proved content in
[critical_chronology_and_retimed_telescope.md](critical_chronology_and_retimed_telescope.md):

- pointwise critical-cut conservation;
- degree conservation in a proper commuting square;
- a local off-critical commuting square, without unauthorized target
  ownership;
- a retimed finite-cylinder covering/embedding pair conditional on an
  independently realized root portrait;
- exact \(z^2-1\) critical-location and critical-time counterexamples;
- the proper-Jordan stagewise degree ladder;
- the quadratic Jordan base-change dichotomy;
- an embedded-Jordan planar critical-spine compiler;
- the exact relative-iterate identity for that planar compiler;
- planar collapse of the specified inverse branches;
- the conditional normalized-coordinate identity;
- the conditional projection-fiber no-go theorem; and
- the corrected fixed-iterate nested-set action.

The packet's broader claims about a source-defined second portrait,
prime-end impression equality, protected end accesses, relative covers,
marked finite-horizon assembly, arbitrary final-root enlargement, and a
complete blow-up category are held open.

No current global open obligation is promoted.  In particular,
`VIRTUAL_DISCONNECTED_MARKING_TRANSFER`, VAAPC-I, UPTCR, and MLC remain
open.

## 5. Decisive finite-word gap

Push II begins its main construction with

\[
 F=f^r:A\longrightarrow B,
 \qquad
 A\Subset B\Subset P.
\tag{5.1}
\]

The virtual problem begins earlier.  It must construct and identify this
data from the actual two-step satellite-copy chain.  Nothing in the packet
proves that (5.1):

1. is the required next virtual portrait rather than an unrelated terminal
   primitive return;
2. has a Jordan neighborhood compactly contained in the direct-decoration
   safe gap
   \[
   \operatorname{int}P\setminus(Q_0\cup Q_1);
   \]
3. contains every component and critical-orbit segment used by the Wave
   proof;
4. carries a marking already satisfying the exact prime-end action
   criterion;
5. has simultaneous component/sheet ownership across every demanded word;
6. is compatible with the multi-base return chain; or
7. persists under the final-root enlargement without new demands or
   collisions.

This gap is recorded explicitly as
`VIRTUAL_FINITE_WORD_ROOT_PORTRAIT_AUTHORIZATION`.

## 6. Telescope audit

### 6.1 Why the original telescope statement is under-specified

Degree-two factorization alone does not preserve degree under base change.
For \(p(z)=z^2:\mathbb D\to\mathbb D\), pull back a small Jordan disk
avoiding \(0\).  Its preimage has two conformal components, so every
connected selected component has degree one.

The propagated critical value must lie in every post-quadratic embedded
base.  Even this is insufficient to infer a Jordan telescope if the
embedded base is not a Jordan subdisk.  A slit domain containing the
critical value gives a connected degree-two preimage with pulled slits,
not a Jordan disk.

The repaired planar theorem therefore requires closure embeddings onto
relatively compact Jordan subdisks and proves only a planar compiler.

### 6.2 Why the relative-cover step remains open

`RELATIVE_COVER_LIFT` is the elementary subgroup criterion for existence of
a lift.  The actual virtual telescope still needs proofs of:

- selected lift embeddedness;
- properness and core-properness;
- compatibility of the selected component through the complete orbit;
- basepoint, subgroup, and deck-phase compatibility;
- lifting and retention of the propagated critical value;
- localization and obstacle separation; and
- protected carrier-complement ideal-boundary action.

Accordingly, the old `MAPPING_TELESCOPE` node is rewritten as this actual
realization gate and remains open.

### 6.3 Why marked assembly remains open

The packet says that conformal embeddings extend as prime-end circle
homeomorphisms.  The statement uses the wrong circle.  For
\(\iota(z)=z/2:\mathbb D\hookrightarrow\mathbb D\), the source prime-end
circle maps to \(|z|=1/2\), not the target prime-end circle.  KKL protected
markings live on ideal boundaries of carrier complements, not merely on
the outer boundary of a Jordan localization.

The exact relative-iterate identity and critical-word concatenation are
proved in `FINITE_PLANAR_TELESCOPE_IDENTITY`.  The existing
`FINITE_HORIZON_ASSEMBLY` obligation remains open for the additional
analytic data.

## 7. End audit

### 7.1 Prime-end coding is not yet proved

There are two missing lemmas before the claimed equality
\(E_\omega=\operatorname{Imp}(\theta_\omega)\).

1. The root puzzle boundary may meet the exterior basin in several ray
   sectors.  One must prove a compatible affine lift of angle doubling on
   every sector used by one word, rather than assume one integer translation
   for the entire angular hull.
2. The actual exterior parts of \(Q_{\omega|m}\) must form a fundamental
   chain cofinal in the prime-end neighborhood filter, including endpoint
   addresses.

Even after impression equality, the access is not “protected” until the
collar, obstacle, cyclic-order, and enlargement audits pass.

### 7.2 What does survive

The planar inverse branches collapse on compact subsets.  The retimed
pairs become exactly the root pair in their own cylinder coordinates.
If a limiting marked space retains a nondegenerate copy of the root carrier
and projects continuously to the original plane, that copy lies in one
projection fiber.

These facts identify a necessary feature of a future end construction.
They do not construct that space.  The explicit residual is
`VIRTUAL_END_BLOWUP_REALIZATION`.

## 8. Code audit

The supplied `critical_spine_audit.py`:

- parses the example;
- correctly implements the integer recurrence
  \[
  \widetilde d_j e_{j+1}=e_jd_j;
  \]
- correctly identifies the supplied shear word;
- reproduces the supplied JSON report; and
- passes Python bytecode compilation.

It is diagnostic only.  In particular:

- `retention` is not aligned to the first quadratic stage;
- its length is not checked against the number of post-quadratic stages;
- a false pre-quadratic flag is reported as a retention failure;
- exactly one quadratic stage is not enforced;
- no properness, Jordan, localization, component, or monodromy hypothesis
  is tested;
- the Boolean flags are not evidence of critical-value containment; and
- it does not audit relative covers, prime ends, obstacles, or enlargement.

The project regressions therefore adopt the arithmetic recurrence and
counterexamples, not the script's geometric interpretation.

## 11. Independent cross-examination

Three independent audits were run in parallel:

1. a finite-word/source-interface audit;
2. a telescope/end audit; and
3. a ledger/schema audit.

They agreed on the decisive conclusions:

- the retimed formula is correct only after the root portrait is supplied;
- the source-facing finite-word realization is not closed;
- the old telescope and finite-horizon nodes must remain open;
- relative-cover and protected-prime-end conclusions are unproved;
- prime-end impression equality is unproved;
- planar collapse and normalized-coordinate identities are valid in their
  narrow scopes; and
- no global status promotion follows.

The disagreements were resolved conservatively.  Where one audit regarded
a conditional marked compiler as promotable and another found missing
prime-end or end-proper definitions, the project adopted only the exact
planar identity and retained the marked theorem as open.

### Second adversarial pass

A fresh line-by-line red team after the first release found eight
write-up defects.  None invalidated a narrow result after its intended
planar/Hausdorff hypotheses were made explicit, but all were repaired
before re-release:

1. the fixed-iterate compact-intersection theorem now assumes Hausdorff
   ambient spaces; the cofinite-topology tail sets give an exact
   counterexample without that hypothesis;
2. the branched quadratic base-change proof now uses Riemann--Hurwitz to
   prove that the connected preimage has one boundary component;
3. the critical-spine failure clause now distinguishes strict exterior
   omission from a boundary-critical collision;
4. the normalized coordinate is the cylinder chart
   \(h_w|_{U_w}:U_w\to A\), not the immersion with codomain \(B\);
5. closed puzzle pieces and open Jordan domains are now typed separately;
6. the telescope identity is stated only on its actual domain
   \(I_m(T_m)\);
7. the \(z^2-1\) shear model now fixes \(0<\rho<1/2\) and proves the final
   branch is a proper conformal map between Jordan domains; and
8. the projection-fiber proof now derives pointwise local noninjectivity
   from the absence of isolated points in a nondegenerate continuum.

The machine statuses were not weakened or promoted by these repairs:
they close omitted hypotheses or proof sentences in the already narrow
tools.  The virtual realization gates remain open.

A separate release-surface audit then found and repaired two status
presentation defects.  The manuscript title “planar protected-spine
compiler” was replaced by “planar critical-spine compiler,” because the
result constructs no protected marking.  The release notes were also
corrected to put
`VIRTUAL_REACHED_CYLINDER_COMPONENT_EXHAUSTION` before its dependent
`VIRTUAL_CYLINDER_TARGET_ADDRESS_SELECTOR`.  These edits change no
machine status; they prevent the title and release summary from claiming
more than the theorem and dependency graph establish.

## 12. Net research advance

The important advance is not a closure of MLC.  It is a sharper boundary:

\[
\begin{gathered}
\text{finite critical chronology is now exactly auditable;}\\
\text{a given root pair has an exact retimed copy on every finite cylinder;}\\
\text{post-quadratic degree retention is exactly critical-value retention;}\\
\text{failure has a first finite component and degree-loss certificate;}\\
\text{unrescaled inverse branches cannot carry a nondegenerate end germ.}
\end{gathered}
\]

The immediate next analytic target is now:

> Construct and source-identify one actual second-scale root portrait in
> the direct-decoration safe gap, with a Wave-complete protected marking
> and exact final-root naturality; then realize its protected critical spine
> in compatible relative covers and construct the nontrivial-fiber/rescaled
> end attachment required by planar collapse.

That target is more precise than the pre-Push-II gate, but it remains a
genuine open construction theorem.

# Unbounded-satellite transitional bridge: source-faithful next theorem

## Scope

This is a new sub-research artifact for the active attempt to prove global
MLC. It does not modify the manuscript or either original PDF.

The question is whether the neutral pseudo-Siegel machinery can be applied to
the unbounded-satellite complement already isolated in the preceding audit.
The answer at this stage is a precise conditional interface, not a completed
theorem.

## Source boundary

Dudko--Luo--Lyubich, *Uniform a priori bounds for neutral renormalization.
Variation II*, arXiv:2509.23031, defines a ψ-bullet-ql Siegel map with a
Siegel disk and covering-embedding conditions for all fiber-product iterates.
Its Theorem 1.3 gives pseudo-Siegel control for that category. The paper's
Theorem 4.7 gives a level-by-level alternative: regularize the next
pseudo-Siegel disk, or obtain an exponential boost in the degeneration. The
source also states that its transitional case has uncertain geometry and that
additional global external input is needed.

Primary source: <https://arxiv.org/html/2509.23031>.

Dudko's MLC survey states that the remaining unbounded satellite problem is
exactly to establish pseudo-Siegel a priori bounds, and that the virtual
Molecule interpolation is a separate problem. See Problems 4.3 and 4.4:
<https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24171>.

## The bridge theorem required for the satellite complement

Let (R_j:U'_j\to U_j) be the selected quadratic-like satellite returns of an
infinitely renormalizable quadratic polynomial, with rational satellite data
(p_j/q_j) and (q_j\to\infty). The missing theorem must construct, with
constants independent of (j), the following package.

### B1. Neutralization with actual domains

For each (j), construct a family of genuine holomorphic maps
(F_{j,\varepsilon}) with a bounded-type Siegel disk and a limit as
(\varepsilon\to0) that recovers the marked satellite return. The construction
must provide the domains, critical point, return time, hybrid normalization,
and the rotation/access marking. Merely matching the multiplier or the
rotation number is not enough.

### B2. ψ-bullet-ql admissibility

The neutralized maps must satisfy the source's full partial-covering package:
degree-two branched covering, properness, Siegel restriction, and the covering
embedding conditions for every fiber-product iterate. These conditions are
what permit the neutral pseudo-Siegel theorem to be invoked.

### B3. Transitional-scale control

At the source's transitional combinatorial level, prove a uniform bound for
the required vertical/peripheral degeneration, or prove that failure creates a
quantified exponential boost that survives the pullback to the original
satellite return. The source's unqualified transitional alternative cannot be
discarded.

### B4. Parameter and marking transfer

Transfer the pseudo-Siegel tilings, protected accesses, and boost/regularization
data back to the satellite parapuzzle with uniformly controlled distortion.
The transfer must preserve the critical chronology and parameter boundaries;
otherwise a dynamical bound does not imply shrinking of the Mandelbrot fiber.

### B5. Infinite assembly

Show that the distortion losses and non-invariance errors over the entire
unbounded tower are summable or telescoping in a declared gauge. This is the
step that would turn level-wise control into a priori bounds and then into
parameter rigidity.

## First unavoidable obstruction

At a rational satellite root, the marked cycle is parabolic rather than a
Siegel cycle. Therefore B1 is not supplied by the neutral theorem. A hybrid
straightening supplies a quadratic polynomial, but not a conformal comparison
on the full pseudo-Siegel nest that simultaneously preserves the critical
orbit, accesses, return chronology, and parameter boundaries. The transition
case in the neutral source is also not automatically resolved by the satellite
rotation denominator.

Consequently, the implication

    satellite rotation p_j/q_j -> neutral pseudo-Siegel bound

is invalid without B1--B5. This is the first source-faithful missing theorem
for the slow-growth complement; it is not a missing finite symbolic label.

## Conditional closure statement

If B1--B5 hold for every realizable unbounded-satellite tower, then the
source's regularization/boost alternative yields uniform pseudo-Siegel or
expanding control at every satellite scale. Combining this with the existing
bounded-type, primitive/puzzle, and rigidity reductions would close the
ordinary unbounded-satellite branch of MLC. The virtual near-Molecule branch
would still require the separate partial-carrier and interpolation theorem.

This conditional implication is a valid target specification; B1--B5 are not
proved here, so the global MLC conjecture remains open in the ledger.

## Verification policy

No numerical pass can verify B1--B5. Code may check the rational rotation
arithmetic and source transcription, but analytic admissibility, marked
transfer, transitional control, and infinite assembly require mathematical
proof. Original documents remain read-only.

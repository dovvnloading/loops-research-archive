## Disposition

The all-period *physical parameter/real-order/degree-two* result has a contiguous audit chain: adjacent dyadic, locally unique solutions for \(3\le p\le12\) and a quantified signed chart with a unique root for \(p\ge13\). The *actual* KKL tree/prime-end identification has a further, identifiable mathematical proof relying on Douady–Hubbard, McMullen, the Misiurewicz theorem, Carathéodory and KKL. The *finite typed* 10/20, attachment, operator, spectral and selected-path statements are consistent **after applying the later arc-length correction**. A physical circle and its two proper restrictions give a harmonic coefficient equality *only in that specific conformal log-strip model*; neither KKL's general restriction lemma nor the finite tables prove an unrestricted Wave equality.

The original request's “one all-period theorem” is consequently defensible only with this **tiered statement**. Claiming unconditional canonical KKL Wave widths, global window uniqueness, all-period four-push width convergence or strict amplification would **overclaim**. No such claim is made in THEOREM.md.

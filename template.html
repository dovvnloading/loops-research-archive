The MLC Conjecture: historical obstructions, current frontier, and computational diagnostics

The Mandelbrot Local Connectivity conjecture remains open. This source-locked dossier traces major changes in the field since 1980, identifies the frontier as of July 2026, and presents reproducible numerical experiments that illustrate central obstructions without treating computation as proof.

Verdict. MLC is unproven but very much alive, and 2026 is the fastest-moving year in its history: two new preprints (January and June) pushed a-priori-bounds territory to the cusp, and Dudko?s ICM 2026 survey reduces what remains to two named problems. The field?s historical errors were never wrong proofs ? none exist, we checked ? but wrong expectations: that it would fall fast, that the dynamical plane would carry the parameter plane, that one renormalization roadmap would cover all combinatorics, and that self-similarity was clean. Each failure localized the difficulty further, and every one of them points at the same address: unbounded satellite combinatorics degenerating toward parabolic cusps. Our own numerics below reproduce that wall quantitatively: convergence is fast precisely where theorems exist and logarithmically slow precisely where they don?t.

?1What the conjecture says, and what it buys

MLC: the Mandelbrot set M ? the set of c ? ? for which the orbit of 0 under z ? z? + c stays bounded ? is locally connected: every point has arbitrarily small connected neighborhoods in M.

Douady?Hubbard proved M is compact, connected and full by building a conformal isomorphism ? : ??M ? ?????. By Carath?odory?Torhorst, MLC is exactly the statement that ??? extends continuously to the boundary circle ? every parameter ray lands, continuously in the angle. Thurston?s quadratic minor lamination builds the quotient this would induce: the pinched disk, an explicitly computable, provably locally connected model space. MLC says M is that model. Schleicher?s fiber formalism localizes it ? MLC ? every ?fiber? (parameters not separated by rational ray pairs) is a single point ? and a trivial fiber literally says the combinatorics of c determines c. That is why experts insist MLC is combinatorial rigidity, not point-set topology.

The payoff chain runs through rigidity to the century-old Fatou conjecture. And by McMullen?s universality theorem, quasiconformal copies of ?M are dense in the bifurcation locus of every nontrivial holomorphic family of rational maps ? settle the topology of M and you?ve set the template for every one-dimensional parameter space in the subject.

One reduction orients everything else: Yoccoz proved MLC at every parameter that is at most finitely renormalizable. So the entire conjecture now lives on the infinitely renormalizable set ? a topologically fat (the boundary has Hausdorff dimension 2, Shishikura) but harmonic-measure-null set of parameters. MLC is already a theorem at harmonic-measure-almost-every boundary point; the open part is invisible to every external observer and enormous in every dimensional gauge. That mismatch is the conjecture?s signature.

?2Historical corrections and obstructions

Ten documented changes of expectation or strategy show how the viable target narrowed over time. They are recorded here for the mathematical constraint each contributed, not as criticism of the original work.

W1

1980 ? Mandelbrot reads his own set wrong verified ? corrected

In the very first publication of the set, Mandelbrot asserted from computer pictures that it was disconnected ? islands of dust. Douady?Hubbard proved connectivity in 1982.

numerics mislead on exactly this class of question; the boundary?s fine structure is below any pixel grid. (See our ?5: harmonic-measure-typical boundary points are ?measurably deep? ? the complement has vanishing area density, so pictures systematically lie.)

W2

1984?1994 ? The founders expect a quick kill verified ? confirmed

The Orsay notes pose MLC alongside the proof that it implies density of hyperbolicity ? ?we do not know how to prove either.? Contemporaries record Douady expecting a fast solution; McMullen wrote in the 1994 Bulletin of the AMS that MLC ?is not too far out of reach.? Thirty-two years later it is still out of reach, and Avila is on record steering students away from the problem.

the conjecture?s difficulty was systematically underestimated by the strongest people in the field, twice a decade, in print.

W3

1980s ? ?Plough in the dynamical plane, harvest in the parameter plane? fails structurally verified ? confirmed

The natural strategy: prove Julia sets locally connected, transfer to M. But the dynamical analogue of MLC is false. Cremer Julia sets are never locally connected (Sullivan?Douady); Herman built non-LC Siegel examples; Douady?Hubbard themselves constructed infinitely renormalizable quadratics with non-LC Julia sets by stacking satellite tunings toward the cusp (written up by Milnor 1992, made explicit by S?rensen 2000). Levin (2011) delivered the punchline: explicit parameters where the Julia set is provably not locally connected while M provably is locally connected right there.

MLC is a parameter-space rigidity statement that survives the total failure of its dynamical counterpart. JLC at c neither implies nor follows from MLC at c.

W4

~1990 ? Yoccoz?s puzzle stops exactly at renormalization

The puzzle method wins at every finitely renormalizable parameter via the Branner?Hubbard criterion: divergent sums of annulus moduli force puzzle pieces to shrink to points. At renormalizable combinatorics the critical tableau becomes periodic, the modulus sum degenerates into a convergent geometric series, and the critical nest converges to a whole small Julia set instead of a point. This is structural, not technical.

local connectivity at infinitely renormalizable points is equivalent to a priori bounds ? uniform lower bounds on renormalization annuli. The problem changed shape here, permanently.

W5

1990s?2000s ? The single-roadmap dream dies: a priori bounds provably FAIL for satellites

The Sullivan?McMullen?Lyubich program assumed one compactness engine would cover all combinatorics. Kahn?Lyubich (2006?09) delivered bounds for primitive combinatorics away from the main molecule ? and then the satellite regime refused. Not for lack of skill: it is now a refereed theorem (Blokh?Levin?Oversteegen?Timorin, Comm. Math. Phys. 2025) that classical polynomial-like a priori bounds fail for satellite renormalization with unbounded relative periods ? moduli are bounded above in terms of the period and must degenerate.

the compactness route that closed the real case cannot close the complex one. Every modern satellite attack (near-parabolic, pacman, pseudo-Siegel) is a designed replacement for a tool that provably does not exist.

W6

1989?2017 ? Naive self-similarity dies twice

Milnor?s 1989 numerics showed Feigenbaum-point magnifications do not converge to a copy of M ? they get ?hairy? (proved by Lyubich 1999; see our render in ?5). Lomonaco?Petersen (2017) proved satellite baby copies are homeomorphic to M but not quasiconformally equivalent to each other.

topological self-similarity coexists with radical metric non-uniformity. Any proof through ?the copies all look alike? is doomed; the copies provably don?t.

W7

1985?2004 ? The degree-2 miracle: Thurston?s model doesn?t generalize

Thurston?s lamination model of M rests on his No Wandering Triangles theorem, and he flagged its generalization as a key question. Answer: false. Blokh?Oversteegen proved wandering triangles exist for cubic laminations.

the clean combinatorial model of M is special to degree two; the conjecture cannot be an instance of a general principle about polynomial parameter spaces ? because the general principle is false (see ?3).

W8

1920s?2012 ? Even the measure-zero folklore fell

The Orsay notes left open the expectation that quadratic Julia sets with empty interior have zero area. Buff?Ch?ritat (2012) constructed positive-area Cremer, Siegel and infinitely-satellite-renormalizable Julia sets ? in exactly the regime where MLC is hard ? completing a reversal that Douady himself initiated. Avila?Lyubich later found positive-area Feigenbaum Julia sets. Meanwhile Fatou?s own implicit expectation (non-hyperbolic maps confined to countably many subvarieties) was already false by Rees?s positive-measure theorem.

the hard set is not thin in any measure-theoretic sense that would let it be ignored; ?pathological? is the generic condition out there.

W9

1992?1997 ? The announcement that took five years to become a theorem

The closest thing to a failed proof in this field?s history: ?wi?tek?s 1992 announced proof of real density of hyperbolicity took until 1997 to appear refereed (Graczyk??wi?tek, with Lyubich?s independent proof). The real case ultimately worked ? real a priori bounds hold for all real combinatorics ? which made it dangerously tempting as a template for ?.

the real line?s order structure, cross-ratio distortion and universal real bounds have no complex analogues; W5 shows the transfer is not merely hard but impossible in the classical form.

W10

2006?2024 ? ?Just extend Kahn?Lyubich? costs a generation

The 2024 Quanta feature documents the human ledger: the satellite wall stalled the a-priori-bounds program for over a decade, damaged careers, and required inventing three new renormalization theories (near-parabolic, pacman, pseudo-Siegel) rather than extending one. Lyubich calls the set ?tremendously devious?; his only committed timeline is a joke about volume 4 of his textbook.

the remaining regime is not a harder instance of the solved regime ? it is a different mathematical object (zero core-entropy, neutral-adjacent, geometric limits enriching the dynamics; see the parabolic-implosion note in ?6).

?3Calibration: one door over, everything fails

The sharpest reason to take MLC?s difficulty seriously ? and to believe it anyway ? is that its exact analogues are refereed-theorem false in every neighboring parameter space.

  • Cubics: Lavaurs proved (1989, via parabolic implosion) that the cubic connectedness locus is not locally connected. Buff?Henriksen: even a one-complex-dimensional cubic slice can have connected, non-locally-connected bifurcation locus.
  • Antiholomorphic (tricorn/multicorns): Hubbard?Schleicher: no multicorn is path connected ? hence none is locally connected. Inou?Mukherjee (Inventiones 2016): rational parameter rays do not land but accumulate on arcs; Misiurewicz points and centers are not dense in the boundary.
  • Straightening: discontinuous in degree ? 3 (Inou) and from baby tricorns to the tricorn (Inou?Mukherjee 2021, settling the Hubbard?Milnor?Schleicher wiggling conjecture).

Every pillar of the quadratic theory ? ray landing, density of Misiurewicz points, continuity of straightening, local connectivity itself ? fails one door over. Whatever proves MLC must exploit what is genuinely special to unicritical quadratic families. Conversely: forty years of searching has found no obstruction mechanism inside M itself, while the mechanisms that kill the neighbors are well understood and provably absent or different in degree 2. That asymmetry is why no expert conjectures MLC false.

The June 2026 paper matters beyond its class: its machinery (Thin-Thick Decomposition of degenerating Riemann surfaces, a Value Calculus on weighted arc diagrams, a Wanderers Theorem, a Wave Lemma) is engineered for unbounded combinatorial growth, and it is reportedly the first MLC class violating the secondary-limbs condition that fenced in every previous result. Its own introduction names what?s left: general primitive near-parabolic combinatorics, general neutral parameters, and ?virtually-satellite? types awaiting virtual-renormalization machinery.

The authoritative roadmap (Dudko, ICM 2026 survey): two problems stand between the field and full a priori bounds ? completing the unbounded satellite cases via pseudo-Siegel technology, and interpolation between positive-entropy and zero-entropy regimes. Behind them sit the two structural conjectures: hyperbolicity of neutral/cylinder renormalization for all rotation numbers, and the Molecule Conjecture ? a single hyperbolic renormalization operator over the whole main molecule. The community signal: a Simons Center workshop ?Half a Century of Dynamical Renormalization? (Feb 2027) organized around exactly this endgame.

How big is what?s left? Graczyk??wi?tek and Smirnov proved that outside an angle set of Hausdorff dimension zero, every parameter ray lands at a Collet?Eckmann (hence finitely renormalizable) parameter: MLC already holds at harmonic-measure-a.e. boundary point. Yet the exceptional set carries the full dimension-2 boundary (Shishikura), contains positive-area Julia set parameters (Avila?Lyubich), and whether ?M itself has positive area is open. The conjecture is simultaneously ?almost everywhere true? and genuinely hard ? the hard set is invisible to every natural measure and dimensionally maximal.

?6Audit of the obscure avenues

The proposed abstract and nonstandard routes separate into three evidentiary tiers.

Tier 1 ? Complete but inert: the reformulations

  • Laminations / pinched disk (Thurston): a finished theory and the definition of success ? but a description of the target, not a weapon. Thurston?s own late program (core entropy, completed by Tiozzo) enriches the model; Dudko?s ICM survey doesn?t mention laminations once.
  • Computability (Penrose?s question): Hertling: MLC ? density of hyperbolicity ? M computable in the bit model. Warning shots exist ? Braverman?Yampolsky?s non-computable Julia sets, and a cubic family whose Mandelbrot-analogue is not Turing computable (Coronel?Rojas?Yampolsky). M may be the only famous planar fractal whose computability is conditional on a dynamical conjecture. Fascinating; supplies zero leverage toward a proof.
  • o-minimality / tame topology: a non-starter for a folklore reason apparently never written down: M?s interior has infinitely many components, and o-minimal definable sets can?t.

Tier 2 ? Powerful next door, blocked at the frontier

  • Self-similar groups / IMG (Nekrashevych, Bartholdi?Dudko): spectacular for postcritically finite combinatorics (twisted rabbit; decidability of Thurston equivalence) ? but the machinery needs expanding dynamics, and the MLC-critical parameters are exactly non-expanding. No published extension to non-PCF rigidity exists.
  • Computer-assisted proof: closer to the action than commonly believed. The lineage runs Lanford ?82 ? Eckmann?Wittwer ? Gaidashev?Yampolsky (Siegel hyperbolicity) ? Lanford?Yampolsky (parabolic renormalization bounds, acknowledged in Dudko?s survey) ? Dudko?Sutherland (dim H(Feigenbaum Julia) < 2, by machine). Every one certifies a single operator; MLC needs bounds uniform over all combinatorics including degenerating limits. Nobody has proposed a machine route to that uniformity in print. This is a real open flank ? see ?7.
  • Formal verification: Geoffrey Irving?s Lean 4 ?ray? project machine-verified the connectedness of M via B?ttcher coordinates ? the deepest formalized holomorphic dynamics anywhere. Local connectivity, puzzles, renormalization: all unformalized.
  • Transcendental dynamics: methodology flows outward, not back; its gift to MLC is a warning ? the Eremenko conjecture, equally plausible-looking, is false (Mart?-Pete?Rempe?Waterman).

Tier 3 ? Live imports: where outside structure actually entered

  • The Sullivan dictionary?s solved half. MLC?s exact Kleinian analogue ? the Ending Lamination Conjecture ? was proved (Minsky; Brock?Canary?Minsky), by precisely the architecture the MLC program now runs: a priori bounds ? bilipschitz combinatorial model ? rigidity, with density (the Fatou analogue: Bers?Sullivan?Thurston density) falling out as a corollary. Minsky?s punctured-torus paper even contains a section literally titled ?Connectivity and a priori bounds.? The transfer is human as much as conceptual: Kahn is a Clay-award hyperbolic geometer, and the 2026 ?Elephant Eyes? preprint imports thick-thin decomposition ? Margulis technology ? into renormalization. The dictionary is heuristic, not a transfer principle; but it is the single most-cited reason experts believe the current program will land.
  • Parabolic implosion (Douady?Lavaurs) is the theory of the wall: near parabolics, renormalization loses compactness because limits are enriched by geometric limits (Lavaurs maps, horn maps, ?calle?Voronin invariants). Inou?Shishikura?s near-parabolic renormalization is literally a normalized horn map; Shishikura?s dimension-2 theorem is implosion?s flagship application. Know the enemy: it has a name and a calculus.
  • New homeomorphic incarnations of M. Petersen?Roesch settled Milnor?s 1993 conjecture: the parabolic Mandelbrot set M? is homeomorphic to M ? via a homeomorphism that is nowhere H?lder. Bullett?Lomonaco: the modular Mandelbrot set of PSL(2,?)-matings is homeomorphic to M?, hence to M. Since local connectivity is a topological invariant, MLC now has three equivalent incarnations, one living in the parabolic world and one on the Kleinian side of the dictionary ? genuinely new terrain for transporting puzzle technology, at the price of the nowhere-H?lder warning (topology transfers; metric control does not).

?7Attack surface: where a new idea could actually bite

Synthesis, honestly framed. Nothing below is a proof strategy; each is a place where the audit shows leverage that is not yet being applied.

  • 1 ? Machine-certified uniformity. The field has computer-assisted bounds for single operators (Feigenbaum, parabolic, golden-mean Siegel) and human-analytic bounds for combinatorial families, but no attempt to machine-certify a bounds statement parametrized over combinatorics ? e.g., interval-arithmetic control of the KKL Value Calculus contraction constant uniformly over a parabolic tail family. The 2026 machinery is explicitly quantitative (Val((f*)?X) ? ???Val(X)); quantitative machinery is what machines certify best. The gap between ?certify one fixed point? and ?certify a compact family of operators? is an engineering frontier, not a conceptual impossibility.
  • 2 ? The Molecule Conjecture as the true target. Every regime-specific theory (puzzles, primitive bounds, near-parabolic, pacman, pseudo-Siegel) is a chart on one atlas; Dudko?s Question 3.1 asks for the single uniformly hyperbolic operator over all of ?M. The historical record indicates that partial compactness engines exist per regime; the missing mathematics is the interpolation between them (positive ? zero entropy). Whoever builds that interpolation ? ?virtual Julia sets? is the survey?s named candidate ? likely finishes the program.
  • 3 ? Transport through the parabolic twin. MLC ? local connectivity of M? ? of M_?. The parabolic incarnation puts the hard regime of M (near-parabolic) at the base of the twin?s structure, where Roesch-style puzzle technology has already proved theorems. Nobody has tried to run the Yoccoz program natively inside M? and pull back. The nowhere-H?lder homeomorphism blocks metric transfer but not combinatorial/topological transfer ? and MLC is topological.
  • 4 ? Formalize the frontier. Irving?s Lean base (B?ttcher theory, connectedness) reaches exactly to the doorstep of puzzle theory. Formalizing the Branner?Hubbard criterion and Yoccoz?s theorem would put the field?s foundation under machine guarantee and ? more practically ? force the modular, quantitative restatement of puzzle arguments that attack #1 needs as input.
  • 5 ? The measure-theoretic pincer. Whether ?M has positive area is open, and the MLC-hard set is precisely where fat Julia sets live (Avila?Lyubich). A proof that the infinitely-renormalizable-satellite parameter set has zero area wouldn?t prove MLC ? but it would collapse the plausible failure modes, and the Graczyk??wi?tek ?measurably deep? technology is active and quantitative.

And the honest counterweight: the people closest to the wall predict the wall falls to the current program ? bounds for unbounded satellites via pseudo-Siegel technology, then rigidity, with the 2027 renormalization workshop already convened around the endgame. The most probable resolution of MLC is not an obscure avenue at all; it is the visible one, finished.
